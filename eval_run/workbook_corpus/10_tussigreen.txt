1. What Tussigreen is used for
Acute/Chronic Bronchitis - Reduces cough severity; comparable to ambroxol in clinical trials - Typical dose: 25 to 50 drops of extract (adults) 2 to 3× daily for 7 to 14 days*
Productive ("Chesty") Cough - Loosens mucus; improves clearance (EMA approved as expectorant)*
Asthma/COPD Support - Increases lung oxygenation in children with asthma*

2. Dosage — Adults

Maximum daily dose: 4 doses/day (4 doses)

Patient education: • Best Time to Use:
-Take oral preparations (syrups/drops) with meals to minimize gastrointestinal discomfort. Dosing typically occurs 3 times daily for acute bronchitis.
• Food/Interaction Precautions:
-Avoid concurrent use of magnesium-containing antacids (may worsen diarrhea). No significant interactions with OTC drugs or supplements reported, but caution advised with other expectorants.
• Missed Dose:
-Skip if close to the next scheduled dose. Do not double dose .
• Overdose Antidote:
-No specific antidote. For accidental ingestion, use activated charcoal and seek medical support for symptomatic management (e.g., antiemetics for vomiting)
