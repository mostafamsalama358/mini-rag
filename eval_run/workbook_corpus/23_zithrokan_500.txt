1. What Zithrokan is used for
Community acquired pneumonia - 500 mg x1, then 250 mg daily x4 days*
Streptococcal pharyngitis - 500 mg x1, then 250 mg x4 days*
Acute otitis media - 30 mg/kg single dose*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 500 mg once daily for 3 days
Maximum daily dose: 1 tablet/day (1 tablet)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 45 kg or adult
Pediatric min age: See pack
Pediatric dose: 10 mg/kg once daily for 3 days or 10 mg/kg day 1 then 5 mg/kg/day days 2-5.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack
