1. What Notussil is used for
Non Productive Cough - Adults: 60 mg PO TID , Children 2 to 14y: 1 mg/kg/dose TID (max 60mg/day)*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 10 mL three times/day
Maximum daily dose: 4 doses/day (4 doses)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 2 years
Pediatric min age: 2 years
Pediatric dose: Children >=2 years: Use age-appropriate dose per physician. Avoid under 2 years.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Notussil if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to LEVODROPROPIZINE* 
Non‑dialysis‑dependent severe Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD Stage 4‑5)* 
Children under 2 years*
