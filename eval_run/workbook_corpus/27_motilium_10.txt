1. What Motilium is used for
Nausea/vomiting - 10 mg PO tid (max 30 mg/day) for ≤1 week *
Gastroparesis - 10 mg tid before meals*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 10 mg up to 3 times/day before meals
Maximum daily dose: 3 tablets/day (3 tablets)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 12 years and ≥35 kg
Pediatric min age: See pack
Pediatric dose: Not routinely recommended in children. Use only if prescribed.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Motilium if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to DOMPERIDONE * Infants under 1 year * Chronic liver disease with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child‑Pugh class B or C) including cirrhosis and decompensation * Atrioventricular (AV) & Heart Blocks (Without Pacemaker)* Cardiac Procedures & Perioperative Pain * Vascular / Aneurysms Heart Diseases * Structural / Valvular & Inflammatory Heart Diseases * Bradyarrhythmias & Sinus Node Dysfunction * Tachyarrhythmias & Hyperactive Conduction * Hyperprolactinemia * Gastrointestinal (GI) bleeding * Obstructive GI Diseases *

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: • None
• but EMA/FDA warnings include:
- QT prolongation & sudden cardiac death (highest risk in age >60, doses >30 mg/day, or with CYP3A4 inhibitors)
- Neuropsychiatric effects (agitation, suicidal ideation) on withdrawal during lactation.
Warnings: • It increases the secretion of the hormone prolactin and thus increases breast milk and reduces fertility in men, gynecomastia, amenorrhea, or impotence (reversible upon decreasing dose or discontinuing drug)
• Take before a meal. 
Take 15-30 minutes before meals
• Avoid:
- CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole, erythromycin) due to increased cardiac risk.
• Caution with:
-Antacids/PPIs (take ≥2h apart to avoid reduced absorption)

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- DOMPERIDONE + ALUMINUM ASPIRIN; Risk: QT prolongation (QTc>450ms); Type: Additive cardiac effects; Monitoring: ECG baseline + weekly; Dose adjustment: Max domperidone 10mg TID; Alternative: Ginger supplements; Patient warning: Palpitation report
- DOMPERIDONE + ACETYL SALICYLIC ACID; Risk: Cardiac arrest; Type: Potassium channel blockade; Monitoring: K+ >4.0 required; Dose adjustment: Max 30mg/day; Alternative: Mosapride; Patient warning: Syncope danger
- DOMPERIDONE + ASPIRIN; Risk: Torsades de Pointes; Type: QTc prolongation (≥500ms); Monitoring: ECG baseline + weekly; Dose adjustment: Max 10mg TID; Alternative: Mosapride; Patient warning: Syncope danger
- DOMPERIDONE + TRIMETAZIDINE; Risk: EPS (SAS score >3).; Type: Pharmacodynamic.; Monitoring: Simpson-Angus Scale weekly.; Dose adjustment: Avoid combination.; Alternative: Ondansetron.; Patient warning: /Management: Report restlessness.
- DOMPERIDONE + DEXTROMETHORPHAN; Risk: Additive CNS depression via dopamine/sigma-1 receptor effects.; Monitoring: Watch for excessive sedation, especially in CYP2D6 poor metabolizers. Dose Adjustment: Limit dextromethorphan to 30mg q6h if combined.; Patient warning: Avoid alcohol and driving. Mechanism: Weak opioid agonist with serotonergic activity.
