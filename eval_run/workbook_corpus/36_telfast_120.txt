1. What Telfast is used for
Allergic Rhinitis - Adults: 60mg BID or 180mg daily , Children 6 to 11y: 30mg BID*
Chronic Urticaria - Adults: 180mg daily , Children 6 to 11y: 30mg BID*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 120 mg once daily
Maximum daily dose: 180 mg/day (180 mg)

Patient education: • Timing: 
-On empty stomach (1h pre- or 2h post-meal); avoid fruit juices.
• Missed Dose: 
-Take ASAP; skip if near next dose.
• Interactions: 
-Take 2h apart from antacids (aluminum/magnesium).
• Overdose Antidote: 
-None (supportive care).
• Seek Help Immediately: 
-Rapid heartbeat, severe dizziness, rash.
