1. What Amaryl is used for
Type 2 diabetes - (1 : 8 mg/day monotherapy or with insulin/metformin)*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 1-2 mg once daily initially
Maximum daily dose: 8 mg/day (8 mg)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 18 years
Pediatric min age: See pack
Pediatric dose: Not recommended in children.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Amaryl if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to GLIMEPIRIDE * Diabetes mellitus- type 1 * Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) * Hypersensitivity to Sulfonamides/ Sulpha drugs*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: • None for glimepiride, 
• but sulfonylureas carry a class warning for increased cardiovascular mortality (based on UGDP study)
Warnings: • Avoid alcohol. Acute and chronic alcohol intake may unpredictably affect the glucose-lowering action of glimepiride
• Avoid with bosentan:
-Contraindicated due to hepatotoxicity risk.
• Herbal interactions: 
-Bitter melon/fenugreek may potentiate hypoglycemia
• Take with food. 
The manufacturer recommends administration with the first meal of the day
• If you are taking colesevelam, take glimepiride at least 4 hours before taking colesevelam
• Check the labels on all your medicines (such as cough-and-cold products) because they may contain ingredients that could affect your blood sugar.

Patient education: • Timing: 
-Take with breakfast to avoid hypoglycemia.
• Missed Dose: 
-Skip if near next dose; never double.
• Hypoglycemia Management: 
-Use 15g fast-acting sugar (e.g., juice) if blood glucose <70 mg/dL.
• Interactions: 
-Avoid alcohol, NSAIDs, and beta-blockers.
