1. What Lamisil is used for
Onychomycosis (Fingernail/Toenail) - 250 mg daily for 6 weeks (fingernails) or 12 weeks (toenails)*
Tinea Pedis (Athlete's Foot), Tinea Cruris (Jock Itch), Tinea Corporis (Ringworm) - Topical cream/gel/spray applied once or twice daily for 1 to 4 weeks*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 250 mg once daily
Maximum daily dose: 250 mg/day (250 mg)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 18 years
Pediatric min age: See pack
Pediatric dose: Oral: Weight-based. Topical: Use according to product labeling.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Lamisil if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to TERBINAFINE * 
Chronic liver disease with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child‑Pugh class B or C) including cirrhosis and decompensation * 
Acute liver failure (including fulminant hepatic necrosis and acute‑on‑chronic liver failure)*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: Terbinafine does not carry a FDA Black Box Warning. 
However, the prescribing information contains strong warnings for:
• Hepatotoxicity:
- Cases of liver failure, some leading to death or liver transplant, have occurred.
• Serious Skin Reactions:
- (e.g., Stevens-Johnson Syndrome, Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis).
• Taste Disturbance:
- Including taste loss, which can be permanent.
Warnings: • Prevent premature labor
• Limit caffeine intake. Terbinafine may reduce the metabolism of caffeine by approximately 19%, 
• Take with or without food
• Avoid Nail Trauma:
- Wear protective footwear in public areas like pools and locker rooms.
• Do Not Share Personal Items:
- Such as nail clippers, files, or towels to prevent spread and reinfection.

Patient education: • Adherence:
- Take the tablet once daily, with or without food. Complete the full course as prescribed, even if the nail starts to look better.
• Time to See Results:
- Nail fungus clears slowly as the healthy nail grows out. It may take 6-12 months after stopping treatment to see the full result for toenails.
• Side Effects to Report Immediately:
- Liver: 
Loss of appetite, nausea, vomiting, unusual tiredness, yellowing of skin/eyes, dark urine.
- Taste/Smell:
 Any change or loss of taste or smell.
- Skin: 
Severe rash or blisters.
- Infection:
 Persistent fever, sore throat.

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- TERBINAFINE + PROPAFENONE; Risk: Mild ↑ propafenone levels (CYP2D6 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: ECG (PR interval), BP; Dose adjustment: None unless CYP2D6 PM; Alternative: Amorolfine nail lacquer; Patient warning: Watch for worsened side effects.
- TERBINAFINE + SOTALOL; Risk: Increased sotalol levels (CYP2D6 inhibition) → QT prolongation risk; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Weekly ECG (QTc), serum electrolytes; Dose adjustment: Reduce sotalol by 25-50%; Alternative: Flecainide (if no structural heart disease)
- TERBINAFINE + METOPROLOL TARTRATE; Risk: 2-4 fold increase in metoprolol exposure (CYP2D6 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Daily BP/HR for first week; Dose adjustment: Reduce metoprolol by 50-75%; Alternative: Atenolol
