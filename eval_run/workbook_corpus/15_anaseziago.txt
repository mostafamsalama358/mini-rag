1. What Anaseziago is used for
Local anesthesia - 0.5 to 2% solutions*
Ventricular tachycardia - 1 to 1.5 mg/kg IV bolus*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: Apply as needed up to 3-4 times/day
Maximum daily dose: 4 capsules/day (4 capsules)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 12 years
Pediatric min age: 12 years
Pediatric dose: Not recommended in children under 12 years.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Anaseziago if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to LIDOCAINE * 
Methemoglobinemia*
Bradyarrhythmias & Sinus Node Dysfunction* 
Atrioventricular (AV) & Heart Blocks (Without Pacemaker)*

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- DIAZOXIDE/LIDOCAINE + RALOXIFENE HYDROCHLORIDE; Risk: Raloxifene's plasma protein binding displacement increases free fractions of both drugs (diazoxide ↑40%, lidocaine ↑35%). Effects: Severe hypotension (SBP <90mmHg), neurotoxicity (lidocaine levels >5mcg/mL), and hyperglycemia (glucose >250mg/dL).; Monitoring: Continuous ECG (QTc), BP q5min, glucose checks.
- See dedicated interaction documents in the workbook corpus for full pairs.
