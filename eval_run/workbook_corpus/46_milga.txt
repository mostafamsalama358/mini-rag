- VITAMIN B6 (PYRIDOXINE) + PRIMIDONE; Risk: Pyridoxine induces metabolism → reduced primidone/phenobarbital levels → loss of seizure control.; Monitoring: Serum primidone (5-12 μg/mL) and phenobarbital levels monthly.; Dose adjustment: Increase primidone 25-50mg/day increments.; Patient warning: Report dizziness/ataxia with dose increases; avoid >100mg/day B6.
