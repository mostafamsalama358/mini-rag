5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: • Risk of serious GI bleeding (especially in elderly)
• Reye's syndrome in children with viral infections
Warnings: • Poor absorption of the drug (Aspirin) in the body, and the reason is that some heart patients use enteric-coated aspirin instead of uncoated aspirin to avoid stomach ulcers
• Avoid alcohol. Alcohol increases the risk of gastrointestinal bleeding.
• Avoid herbs and supplements with anticoagulant/antiplatelet activity. Examples include garlic, ginger, bilberry, danshen, piracetam, and ginkgo biloba.May increase bleeding risk
• Take after a meal. This reduces irritating gastrointestinal effects.
• Take with a full glass of water.
• Do not lie down for at least 10 minutes after you have taken this drug. If stomach upset occurs while you are taking this medication, you may take it with food or
