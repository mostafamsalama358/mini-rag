Folate Deficiency - 1 : 5 mg/day PO*
NTD Prevention - 400 : 800 µg/day pre conception → 12w gestation*

Patient education: • Timing: 
-With/without food; consistent daily.
• Missed Dose: 
-Take ASAP; skip if near next dose.
• Interactions:
- Take 2h apart from zinc, antacids, cholestyramine.
• Overdose Antidote: 
-None (excess excreted renally).
• Seek Help Immediately: 
-Rash, wheezing, neurological changes (numbness/balance issues).

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- FOLIC ACID + THIOPENTAL; Risk: ↓ Antiepileptic efficacy (enhanced metabolism) → breakthrough seizures. Monitor: Seizure frequency, EEG if indicated. Dose Adjust: ↑ Thiopental dose by 20-30% if levels subtherapeutic.
- FOLIC ACID + PHENOBARBITAL; Risk: ↓ Phenobarbital levels → seizure recurrence (CYP induction by folate). Monitor: Phenobarbital levels q1-2mo, seizure diary. Dose Adjust: ↑ Phenobarbital by 25% if levels <15 mcg/mL.
- FOLIC ACID + PHENOBARBITONE; Risk: ↓ Antiepileptic efficacy → loss of seizure control. Monitor: Serum levels (target 15-40 mcg/mL), breakthrough seizures. Dose Adjust: ↑ Phenobarbitone by 5-10mg/wk guided by levels.
- FOLIC ACID + PRIMIDONE; Risk: ↓ Primidone/phenobarbital levels → increased seizure risk. Monitor: Phenobarbital levels (from primidone metabolism), seizure frequency. Dose Adjust: ↑ Primidone by 25% if phenobarbital <15 mcg/mL.
- FOLIC ACID + AMOBARBITAL; Risk: Reduced antiepileptic effect → status epilepticus risk. Monitor: Amobarbital levels (if available), seizure clusters. Dose Adjust: ↑ Amobarbital by 30-50mg/day if control wanes.
- See dedicated interaction documents in the workbook corpus for full pairs.
