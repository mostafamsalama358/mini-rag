1. What Lipitor is used for
Hyperlipidemia - Reduce elevated TC, LDL C, apoB, TG. Start 10 : 20 mg once daily; max 80 mg. Starting dose in patients requiring larger LDL C reduction (ie, >45%): 40 mg PO qDay , Maintenance: 10 : 80 mg PO qDay, check lipid levels after 2 : 4 weeks and adjust dose accordingly*
Hypertriglyceridemia, Adjunct to diet for elevated TG levels - 10 mg PO qDay initially , Maintenance: 10 to 80 mg PO qDay maintenance, check lipid levels after 2 : 4 weeks and adjust dose accordingly*
Reduction of risk of stroke, heart attack, and revascularization procedures in patients without evidence of coronary heart disease (CHD) but with multiple risk factors other than diabetes (eg, smoking, HTN, low HDL C, family history of early CHD)-10 : 80 mg PO qDay*
Primary Prevention - Reduce CV risk in adults with multiple risk factors*
Secondary Prevention- Reduce risk of MI, stroke in adults with CVD*

• Pregnancy: Do not use if pregnant or trying to become pregnant.

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- ATORVASTATIN + AMIODARONE; Risk: ↑ Atorvastatin levels → myopathy; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: LFTs/CPK; Dose adjustment: Max 20mg daily; Alternative: Rosuvastatin; Patient warning: /Management: Avoid strenuous exercise.
- ATORVASTATIN + DRONEDARONE; Risk: ↑ Atorvastatin levels (CYP3A4 inhibition) → myopathy; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: CPK if muscle symptoms; Dose adjustment: Max 20mg daily; Alternative: Rosuvastatin; Patient warning: /Management: Report unexplained muscle pain.
- ATORVASTATIN + DIAZOXIDE; Risk: Exacerbated hyperglycemia; Type: Additive metabolic effect; Monitoring: Glucose HbA1c q3mo, LFTs; Dose adjustment: Usually none needed; Alternative: Pravastatin
- ATORVASTATIN + BOSENTAN; Risk: Reduced atorvastatin efficacy (CYP3A4 induction) & hepatotoxicity; Type: Pharmacokinetic & additive toxicity; Monitoring: Lipid panel, LFTs monthly; Dose adjustment: ↑ Atorvastatin dose likely; Alternative: Fluvastatin
- ATORVASTATIN + MINOXIDIL; Risk: Orthostatic hypotension; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: Sitting/standing BP, CK; Dose adjustment: Minoxidil dose reduction may be needed; Alternative: Pravastatin
- See dedicated interaction documents in the workbook corpus for full pairs.
