1. What Diflucan is used for
Oropharyngeal Candidiasis - 200 mg day 1, then 100 mg daily × 7 : 14d*
Vaginal Candidiasis - 150 mg × 1 dose*
Cryptococcal Meningitis - 400 : 800 mg daily × 10 : 12w (induction)*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 150 mg single dose
Maximum daily dose: 400 mg/day (400 mg)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 18 years
Pediatric min age: See pack
Pediatric dose: 3-12 mg/kg/day depending on indication.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Diflucan if / disease cautions
Infants under 6 months * Hypersensitivity to AZOLES (ITRACONAZOLE/FLUCONAZOLE/VORICONAZOLE...) * First trimester pregnancy * Long QT syndrome (congenital or acquired)*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: • HEPATIC TOXICITY & QT PROLONGATION:
"May cause severe hepatotoxicity. Monitor LFTs. Avoid in congenital long QT syndrome or with other QT-prolonging drugs."
Warnings: • Take this medication by mouth usually as a single dose, with or without food at any time of the day
• All azole antifungal medicines: cause liver problems and cause heart rhythm disturbances because they prolong the QT except fluconazole, which is excreted through the kidneys
• It increases the level of INR in the patient who uses warfarin, and the most dangerous of them is fluconazole, ketoconazole, voriconazole, so it is necessary to monitor the INR level well.
• Avoid:
- St. John’s wort (↓ fluconazole levels via CYP3A4 induction).
- Alcohol (↑ hepatotoxicity risk)

Patient education: • Timing: 
-With/without food; consistent daily timing.
• Missed Dose: 
-Take ASAP; skip if near next dose.
• Interactions:
- Avoid alcohol, blood thinners, cisapride; report supplements.
• Overdose Antidote: 
-None (hemodialysis removes 50%).
• Seek Help Immediately: 
-Dark urine, yellow skin/eyes, dizziness/fainting, irregular heartbeat.

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- FLUCONAZOLE + AMIODARONE; Risk: Sudden cardiac death (QTc >550ms); Type: CYP3A4 + hERG inhibition; Monitoring: LFTs + QTc weekly; Dose adjustment: Contraindicated; Alternative: Isavuconazonium; Patient warning: Avoid grapefruit.
