1. What Duphaston is used for
Luteal phase support - 10 mg BID starting at ovulation*
Dysmenorrhea - 10 mg BID days 5 : 25 of cycle*
Endometriosis - 10 mg BID , TID continuously*
HRT combination - 5 : 10 mg/day with estrogen*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: Depends indication; commonly 10 mg 1-2 times/day
Maximum daily dose: 30 mg/day (30 mg)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 18 years
Pediatric min age: See pack
Pediatric dose: Not indicated in pediatric patients.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Duphaston if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to DYDROGESTERONE * Increased Intracranial Pressure (ICP)* Abnormal uterine/ Vaginal bleeding (AUB)* Chronic liver disease with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child‑Pugh class B or C) including cirrhosis and decompensation*
