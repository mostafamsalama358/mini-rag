1. What Claritine is used for
Allergic rhinitis - 10 mg/day (adults); 5 mg/day (ages 2 to 5)*
Chronic urticaria - 10 mg/day*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 10 mg once daily
Maximum daily dose: 10 mg/day (10 mg)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 2 years
Pediatric min age: 2 years
Pediatric dose: 2-12 years: <30 kg: 5 mg once daily. >=30 kg: 10 mg once daily.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Claritine if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to LORATADINE * Phenylketonuria (PKU) * Children under 2 years *

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- LORATADINE + ANASTRAZOLE; Risk: CYP3A4/2D6 dual inhibition → anastrozole↑15%.; Monitoring: Minimal - no dose adjustment needed.; Patient warning: Dry mouth may occur.
- LORATADINE + DEXTROMETHORPHAN; Risk: Cognitive impairment; Interaction: Subtle CNS synergy; Monitoring: Digit symbol test; Dose adjustment: Space doses 6h apart; Alternative: Beclomethasone nasal
- LORATADINE + PENTAZOCINE; Risk: Dizziness; Type: Additive; Monitoring: Orthostatic BP; Dose adjustment: Usually none; Alternative: Desloratadine; Patient warning: Rise slowly.
- LORATADINE + TRAMADOL; Risk: Cognitive slowing; Interaction: Subtle CNS effects; Monitoring: Digit symbol test; Dose adjustment: Space doses 8h apart; Alternative: Fluticasone nasal
- LORATADINE + CODEINE; Risk: Cognitive impairment; Interaction: Subtle CNS synergy; Monitoring: Digit symbol test; Dose adjustment: Space doses 8h apart; Alternative: Fluticasone nasal
- See dedicated interaction documents in the workbook corpus for full pairs.
