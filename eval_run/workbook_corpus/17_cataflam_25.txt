1. What Cataflam is used for
Osteoarthritis/Rheumatoid Arthritis - 50 mg TID or 75 mg BID*
Acute Pain/Primary Dysmenorrhea - 50 mg TID (max 200 mg/day)*
Migraine - 50 mg PRN (with aura)*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 1 tablet 2-3 times/day
Maximum daily dose: 150 mg/day (150 mg/day)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 14 years
Pediatric min age: See pack
Pediatric dose: 0.5-2 mg/kg/day in 2-3 divided doses. Max 150 mg/day.
Pediatric max daily dose: 150 mg/day

4. Do not take Cataflam if / disease cautions
Infants under 1 year * Specific Coagulopathies & Platelet Disorders * Asthma & Reactive Airway Diseases * Hypersensitivity to NSAIDs/ASPIRIN * Coronary Artery Disease & Ischemic Heart Disease * Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD) * Peptic Ulcer Disease & Gastritis * GI Perforation / Ischemia & Bleeding * GERD & Esophageal Disorders * Gastrointestinal (GI) bleeding * Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) * Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis (TEN) * Non‑dialysis‑dependent severe Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD Stage 4‑5) * Chronic liver disease with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child‑Pugh class B or C) including cirrhosis and decompensation* Solitary kidney* Cardiac Procedures & Perioperative Pain*

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- DICLOFENAC POTASSIUM + PROPAFENONE; Risk: CYP2C9 inhibition → ↑ propafenone levels (arrhythmia risk) + GI bleeding; Type: Pharmacokinetic (CYP2C9 competition) + Pharmacodynamic (antiplatelet); Monitoring: ECG (PR/QRS), Hgb, renal function; Dose adjustment: Max 75 mg/day diclofenac; Alternative: Topical diclofenac; Patient warning: Report palpitation…
- DICLOFENAC POTASSIUM + CAPTOPRIL; Risk: Hyperkalemia and AKI (higher with potassium salt); Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: K⁺, Cr (baseline + 3 days); Dose adjustment: Reduce diclofenac frequency; Alternative: Diclofenac topical gel; Patient warning: /Management: Avoid potassium supplements; report muscle weakness.
- DICLOFENAC POTASSIUM + ENALAPRIL; Risk: Hyperkalemia (potassium salt) + AKI; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: K⁺, Cr (baseline + 3 days); Dose adjustment: Avoid in CKD; max 75 mg/day; Alternative: Diclofenac topical; Patient warning: /Management: Avoid potassium supplements.
- DICLOFENAC POTASSIUM + LISINOPRIL; Risk: Hyperkalemia (potassium salt) + AKI; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: K⁺, Cr (baseline + 3 days); Dose adjustment: Avoid in CKD; max 75 mg/day; Alternative: Diclofenac topical; Patient warning: /Management: Avoid potassium supplements.
- DICLOFENAC POTASSIUM + RAMIPRIL; Risk: Hyperkalemia and AKI; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: K+, Cr (baseline + 3 days); Dose adjustment: Avoid if CKD; max 75 mg/day; Alternative: Topical diclofenac; Patient warning: /Management: Avoid potassium-rich foods.
