1. What Antinal is used for
Acute bacterial diarrhea - 200mg 4x/day (adults); 100mg 4x/day (children 6+ years)*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 1 capsule 4 times/day
Maximum daily dose: 8 capsules/day (8 capsules)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 6 years
Pediatric min age: 2 years
Pediatric dose: >=2 years: 200 mg every 6 hours.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Antinal if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to NIFUROXAZIDE* Infants under 6 months *
