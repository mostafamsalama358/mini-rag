-With/without food; bedtime dosing for nocturnal symptoms.

-Take ASAP; skip if near next dose.
• Interactions: 
-Avoid antacids within 1h; limit alcohol (↑ GI irritation).
• Overdose Antidote: 
-None. Supportive care.
• Seek Help: 
-Bloody stools, racing heart, confusion, rash.
