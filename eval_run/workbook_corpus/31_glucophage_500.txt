Patient education: • Timing: 
-With meals to reduce GI upset
• SUPPLEMENTS TO AVOID/CAUTION:
- Berberine: AVOID concurrent use. Similar glucose-lowering mechanism; risk of additive hypoglycemia.
- High-Dose Bitter Melon/ Cinnamon: Use with caution and glucose monitoring. May potentiate hypoglycemia.
- Chromium: Evidence is mixed. If used, limit to 200-1000 mcg daily with MD supervision.
• Dosing: 
-Take exactly as prescribed (do not crush XR)
• Missed dose: 
-Skip if >6h late; never double
• Stop & seek help: 
-Muscle pain, trouble breathing, dizziness (lactic acidosis signs)
