1. What Strepsils is used for
It’s an antiseptic found in lozenges (e.g., Strepsils®) used for sore throats -Its role: local antiseptic effect in the mouth and throat*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 1 lozenge every 2-3 hours; max 12/day
Maximum daily dose: 12 lozenges/day (12 lozenges)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 6 years
Pediatric min age: 6 years
Pediatric dose: Not recommended under 6 years.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Strepsils if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to AMYLMETACRESOL * Children under 6 years * Non-Acute / Cutaneous Porphyrias *

Patient education: • Timing:
- Dissolve one lozenge slowly in your mouth every 2-3 hours as needed. Do not chew or swallow whole.
- Not for children <6 years
• Administration:
- Allow the lozenge to dissolve slowly to maximize contact time with the throat.
• Missed Dose:
- Not applicable. Use as needed for symptoms.
• Storage:
- Store at room temperature.
• Important Note:
- This is for temporary relief. If symptoms persist after 2-3 days, see a doctor. Do not use for more than 3 days without medical advice.
