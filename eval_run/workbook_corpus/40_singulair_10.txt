1. What Singulair is used for
Asthma prophylaxis - 10 mg daily (adults); 5 mg (6 to 14y); 4 mg (2 to 5y)*
Allergic rhinitis - 10 mg daily: If Patients with both asthma and allergic rhinitis should take only 1 dose qPM*
Exercise induced bronchospasm - 10 mg 2h pre exercise*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 10 mg once daily evening
Maximum daily dose: 10 mg/day (10 mg)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 15 years
Pediatric min age: 6 months
Pediatric dose: 6 months-5 years: 4 mg daily. 6-14 years: 5 mg daily. >=15 years: 10 mg daily.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Singulair if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to MONTELUKAST * Psychotic Disorders * Mood Disorders (Depression & Bipolar) * Chronic liver disease with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child‑Pugh class B or C) including cirrhosis and decompensation* Phenylketonuria (PKU)*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: Serious neuropsychiatric events that may include suicidal thoughts or actions have been reported in patients taking montelukast, If changes in behavior are observed, or aggression, depression, and sleep disturbances reported, advise patients to discontinue montelukast, especially in children. This Events have occurred in patients with and without pre-existing psychiatric disease

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- MONTELUKAST + THIOPENTAL; Risk: THIOPENTAL decreases MONTELUKAST levels by 30% via CYP3A4 induction. Monitor: Asthma control tests.; Dose adjustment: MONTELUKAST may need 50% dose increase.; Patient warning: Watch for worsening wheezing.
- MONTELUKAST + PHENOBARBITAL; Risk: PHENOBARBITAL decreases MONTELUKAST levels by 40% via CYP3A4 induction. Monitor: Asthma control test scores monthly.; Dose adjustment: Increase MONTELUKAST to 15mg nightly if >15yo.; Patient warning: Watch for nocturnal asthma symptoms.
- MONTELUKAST + PHENOBARBITONE; Risk: PHENOBARBITONE reduces MONTELUKAST efficacy by 50%. Monitor: Fractional exhaled nitric oxide (FeNO) levels.; Dose adjustment: Consider alternative leukotriene modifier.; Patient warning: Increased rescue inhaler use may be needed.
- MONTELUKAST + PRIMIDONE; Risk: PRIMIDONE (via phenobarbital) decreases MONTELUKAST AUC by 60%. Monitor: Morning/evening peak flow variability.; Dose adjustment: MONTELUKAST may need 2x daily dosing.; Patient warning: Report increased wheezing at night.
- MONTELUKAST + AMOBARBITAL; Risk: Reduced montelukast efficacy (CYP3A4/CYP2C9 induction) worsening asthma control. Monitor: Asthma symptoms, PEFR/FEV1, rescue inhaler use.
