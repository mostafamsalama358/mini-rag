1. What Brufen is used for
Pain/fever - 200 : 400mg q4 : 6h (max 1200mg/day OTC; 3200mg/day Rx)*
RA/OA - 400 : 800mg TID (max 2400 : 3200mg/day)*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 1 tablet every 6-8 hours; max 1200-2400 mg/day
Maximum daily dose: 2400 mg/day (2400 mg/day)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 12 years
Pediatric min age: See pack
Pediatric dose: 20-30 mg/kg/day in 3-4 divided doses. Max 40 mg/kg/day.
Pediatric max daily dose: 40 mg/kg/day

4. Do not take Brufen if / disease cautions
Infants under 6 months *
Hypersensitivity to NSAIDs/ASPIRIN *
Specific Coagulopathies & Platelet Disorders *
Asthma & Reactive Airway Diseases * 
Acute liver failure (including fulminant hepatic necrosis and acute‑on‑chronic liver failure) * 
Chronic liver disease with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child‑Pugh class B or C) including cirrhosis and decompensation *
Liver tumours (malignant and benign) * 
Non‑dialysis‑dependent severe Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD Stage 4‑5) * 
Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) * 
Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis (TEN)* 
Third trimester pregnancy*
Coronary Artery Disease & Ischemic Heart Disease * 
Solitary kidney*
Cardiac Procedures & Perioperative Pain*
