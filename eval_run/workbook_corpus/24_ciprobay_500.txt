6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- CIPROFLOXACIN + CLOPIDOGREL; Risk: Decreased clopidogrel activation (CYP2C19 inhibition).; Type: Pharmacokinetic.; Monitoring: Therapeutic efficacy (e.g., recurrent CV events).; Dose adjustment: Avoid high-dose ciprofloxacin (>750mg BID).; Alternative: Levofloxacin or non-fluoroquinolone antibiotics.; Patient warning: /Management: Monitor for treatm…
- CIPROFLOXACIN + DULOXETINE; Risk: ↑ Duloxetine levels (CYP1A2 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP, serotonin syndrome signs; Alternative: Levofloxacin; Patient warning: Discontinue if hypertensive crisis occurs.
- CIPROFLOXACIN + PHENYTOIN; Risk: Ciprofloxacin ↑ phenytoin levels (moderate CYP2C9 inhibition).; Monitoring: Phenytoin levels within 3 days.; Dose adjustment: ↓ phenytoin by 20-30% if combined >3 days.; Patient warning: Watch for nausea or diplopia.
- CIPROFLOXACIN + ZOLMITRIPTAN; Risk: Possible reduced zolmitriptan efficacy; Monitoring: Document migraine attack duration/severity; Dose adjustment: Adjust dose based on clinical response; Patient warning: Avoid missing doses due to interaction risk.
- CIPROFLOXACIN + ROPINIROLE; Risk: CYP1A2 inhibition ↑ ropinirole exposure → hallucinations, syncope, and impulse control disorders; Monitoring: Neuropsychiatric eval weekly, orthostatic BP; Dose adjustment: Halve ropinirole dose if coadministered >3 days
