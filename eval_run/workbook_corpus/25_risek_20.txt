1. What Risek is used for
Healing of Erosive Esophagitis - 20 mg daily for 4 to 8 weeks*
Maintenance of Healing - 20 mg daily*
Symptomatic GERD - 20 mg daily for 4 weeks*
Risk Reduction of NSAID Associated Gastric Ulcer - 20 mg daily for up to 6 months*
H. pylori Eradication - 20 mg BID as part of combination therapy (e.g., with amoxicillin and clarithromycin) for 10 to 14 days*
Pathological Hypersecretory Conditions (e.g., Zollinger Ellison syndrome) - Starting dose 60 mg daily; adjust based on acid output. Doses >80 mg daily should be divided*
Duodenal Ulcer - 20 mg daily for 4 to 8 weeks*

- Take before a meal, typically 30-60 minutes before breakfast. For twice-daily dosing, take before the morning and evening meals.
- Swallow delayed-release capsules/tablets whole; do not crush, chew, or break them.
- For oral suspension:
 Empty the packet into a small cup containing 1-2 tablespoons of water. Stir and drink immediately. Refill with water, stir, and drink again to ensure full dose.
