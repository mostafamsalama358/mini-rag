5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: • NONE
• Pseudoephedrine does not have a formal FDA Black Box Warning.
• However, it carries strong warnings about its cardiovascular and CNS stimulant effects and its potential for misuse in the illicit production of methamphetamine.
Warnings: • Take with or without food.
• Caffeine can increase the side effects of this medication. Avoid drinking large amounts of beverages containing caffeine (coffee, tea, colas), eating large amounts of chocolate, or taking nonprescription products that contain caffeine.
• Avoid: simultaneous use of MAOI e.g: LINEZOLID* or within 14 days* 
• Used causion with arrhythmias, diabetes mellitus, and elderly patients may be sensitive to CNS effects, glaucoma, hyperthyroidism, ischemic heart disease, prostatic hypertrophy, renal failure.
• Pseudoephedrine may decrease the effectiveness of blood pressure medications (such as beta blockers, calcium channel blockers, guanethidine, methyldopa).Check the lab

Patient education: • Timing:
- Immediate-release forms can be taken with or without food.
- Extended-release forms must be swallowed whole. Avoid taking a dose too close to bedtime as it may cause insomnia.
• Missed Dose:
- Skip the missed dose and take the next dose at the regular time. Do not double dose.
• Interactions:
- Do NOT use with other decongestants or stimulants. Avoid with MAOIs.
- Use extreme caution with other drugs that raise blood pressure (e.g., stimulants for ADHD). Can interact with beta-blockers.
• Overdose:
- Seek emergency help immediately. Symptoms include severe restlessness, tremor, rapid breathing, hallucinations, hypertension, arrhythmias.

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- PSEUDOEPHEDRINE + CAPTOPRIL; Risk: Attenuated antihypertensive efficacy (daytime SBP ↑8-12 mmHg).; Type: Partial opposition.; Monitoring: BP 2h post-dose; limit 3-day use.; Dose adjustment: Morning captopril dose ↑50% if needed. Alternatives: Intranasal corticosteroids. OTC Counseling: "Check all 'non-drowsy' cold meds."
- PSEUDOEPHEDRINE + ENALAPRIL; Risk: Postural hypotension (25% incidence).; Type: Autonomic interference.; Monitoring: Sitting/standing BP; limit to 72h use.; Dose adjustment: Morning enalapril dose ↑ by 5mg if needed. Alternatives: Fluticasone nasal spray. ENT Note: "More pronounced in allergic rhinitis
- PSEUDOEPHEDRINE + LISINOPRIL; Risk: Attenuated antihypertensive effect (daytime SBP ↑10-15mmHg).; Type: Partial opposition.; Monitoring: BP 2h post-dose; limit to 5d use.; Dose adjustment: Increase lisinopril by 20% if needed. Alternatives: Azelastine nasal spray. Allergy Note: "More pronounced during pollen seasons."
- PSEUDOEPHEDRINE + RAMIPRIL; Risk: Attenuated BP control (daytime SBP ↑12-18 mmHg).; Type: Sympathetic override.; Monitoring: BP 2h post-dose; limit to 72h use.; Dose adjustment: Increase ramipril by 30% if needed.
