1. What Smecta is used for
Acute Diarrhea in Adults & Children - Adult: 1 sachet (3g) three times daily , Children >2 years: 4 : 6 sachets per day (dose decreases with age and weight), Infants (<1 year): 2 sachets per day (under medical supervision)*
Symptomatic Treatment of Esophagitis & Gastritis - Dose: 1 sachet (3g) three times daily after meals*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 3 sachets/day
Maximum daily dose: 6 sachets/day (6 sachets)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: Any age
Pediatric min age: Infancy
Pediatric dose: <1 year: 1 sachet/day. 1-2 years: 1-2 sachets/day. >2 years: 2-3 sachets/day.
Pediatric max daily dose: 3 sachets/day

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: • NONE

• Medical Info:
- Risks:
 Constipation. Potential for heavy metal contamination in non-pharmaceutical grade products.
- Mechanism:
 Has a high adsorption capacity. Acts by binding to and inactivating digestive toxins, viruses, and bacteria. It also forms a protective physical barrier on the gastrointestinal mucosa.
- Stop Use: 
Severe constipation, absence of bowel movements, or if symptoms worsen.
- Withdrawal:
 No withdrawal syndrome.
- Not recommended for children under 2 years of age without medical advice

Patient education: • Timing:
- For diarrhea, it is best taken between meals. For esophagitis/gastritis, it is taken after meals. Follow product instructions.
• Administration:
- Empty the contents of the sachet into a glass of water (approx. 50 mL for adults). Stir until a slurry forms and drink it immediately. It can also be mixed with semi-liquid food (e.g., porridge, compote) for children.
• Missed Dose:
- Take as soon as remembered. If it is almost time for the next dose, skip the missed dose. Do not double dose.
• Interactions:
- Can adsorb other drugs in the GI tract, reducing their absorption and efficacy. Administer other medications at least 2 hours before or after taking dioctahedral smectite.
