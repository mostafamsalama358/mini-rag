1. What Flagyl is used for
Anaerobic infections - 500 mg IV/PO q6 to 8h*
Amebiasis - 500 to 750 mg PO q8hr for 5 to 10 days & Pediatric: 35 to 50 mg/kg/day in 3 divided doses x 10 days*
C. difficile - 500 mg PO TID x10 to 14d*
Trichomoniasis - 2g PO single dose*
Bacterial vaginosis - Topical 0.75% gel 5g qd x5d. or Nonpregnant women 500 mg PO BID x 7 days OR 2 g PO qDay single dose, Pregnant women 500 mg PO BID x 7 days, OR 250 mg PO TID x 7 days*
Sexually Transmitted Disease , Prevention following sexual assault - 2 g PO as a single dose; 3 drug regimen that also includes ceftriaxone or cefixime, PLUS azithromycin or doxycycline*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 500 mg every 8-12 hours
Maximum daily dose: 4 tablets/day (4 tablets)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 10 years
Pediatric min age: See pack
Pediatric dose: 20-30 mg/kg/day in divided doses. Depends on indication.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Flagyl if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to NITROIMIDAZOLES (METRONIDAZOLE/TINIDAZOLE/SECNIDAZOLE) * Young infants (<2 months) * First trimester pregnancy * Cockayne syndrome*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: • None (Note: Carcinogenicity observed in rodents; avoid unnecessary use).
Warnings: • Changes the color of the skin, urine, or stool to Brown/Yellow

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- METRONIDAZOLE + LITHIUM CARBONATE; Risk: Lithium toxicity (reduced renal clearance); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Lithium levels day 3 and 5; Dose adjustment: Reduce lithium by 30%; Alternative: Vancomycin for C.diff; Patient warning: /Management: Report metallic taste/ataxia.
- METRONIDAZOLE + THIOPENTAL; Risk: ↓ Metronidazole efficacy + disulfiram-like reaction with IV thiopental.; Monitoring: Ethanol screen, neurologic status.; Dose adjustment: Avoid ethanol-containing products; ↑ metronidazole 33%.; Patient warning: No alcohol 72h post-treatment.
- METRONIDAZOLE + PHENOBARBITAL; Risk: ↓ Metronidazole efficacy + ↑ neurotoxicity.; Monitoring: Neurologic exam daily.; Dose adjustment: ↑ Metronidazole 50% or use IV formulation.; Patient warning: Report numbness/ataxia immediately.
- METRONIDAZOLE + PHENOBARBITONE; Risk: ↓ Metronidazole efficacy + ↑ neurotoxicity risk.; Monitoring: Daily neurologic exams.; Dose adjustment: ↑ Metronidazole 50% or use IV route.; Patient warning: Absolute alcohol avoidance for 96h.
- METRONIDAZOLE + PRIMIDONE; Risk: Seizure threshold lowering + ↓ antibiotic efficacy.; Monitoring: EEG if high doses.; Dose adjustment: Avoid combination in epilepsy; Patient warning: Report myoclonus immediately.
