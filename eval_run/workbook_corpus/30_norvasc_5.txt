1. What Norvasc is used for
Hypertension - Adults: Start 5 mg daily; max 10 mg daily. Pediatrics (≥6 years): 2.5 : 5 mg daily*
Chronic Stable Angina - 5 : 10 mg daily*
Vasospastic Angina - 5 : 10 mg daily*
CAD - 5 : 10 mg daily to reduce hospitalization/revascularization*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 5 mg once daily
Maximum daily dose: 1 tablet/day (1 tablet)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 6 years
Pediatric min age: 6 years
Pediatric dose: 6-17 years: 2.5-5 mg once daily.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Norvasc if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to AMLODIPINE *
Severe Hypotension *
Structural / Valvular & Inflammatory Heart Diseases*
Heart Failure (CHF)*
Chronic liver disease with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child‑Pugh class B or C) including cirrhosis and decompensation*
Pregnancy; unspecified gestational age*
Breastfeeding*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: None for amlodipine. However, critical warnings include:
• Angina/MI Exacerbation: Rare worsening of ischemia at initiation or dose escalation, especially in severe CAD.
• Hypotension: Risk in volume-depleted or aortic stenosis patients
Warnings: • Avoid grapefruit products 
• Using amlodpine together with multivitamin with minerals can decrease the effects of amlodpine , You may need a dose adjustment or need your blood pressure checked more often if you take both medications (Calcium-containing products may decrease the effectiveness of calcium channel blockers by saturating calcium channels with calcium.
