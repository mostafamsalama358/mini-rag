1. What Concor is used for
Hypertension - Initial dose 5 mg once daily. May be increased to 10 mg, and then up to 20 mg once daily if needed*
Chronic Heart Failure (NYHA Class II , III) - Must be initiated at a very low dose and titrated slowly. Initial: 1.25 mg once daily. Target: 10 mg once daily, if tolerated*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 5 mg once daily
Maximum daily dose: 1 tablet/day (1 tablet)

Patient education: Do not stop taking this medicine suddenly. This can cause dangerous heart problems. Your doctor will tell you how to reduce the dose gradually if you need to stop.

Monitor Your Pulse: Learn how to check your heart rate. If it drops below 50-55 beats per minute (or as directed by your doctor) and you feel dizzy or tired, contact your doctor.

For Diabetics: This drug can mask the fast heartbeat you feel when your blood sugar is low. Be extra vigilant about monitoring your blood glucose levels.

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- BISOPROLOL + AMIODARONE; Risk: ↑ Bisoprolol levels → exercise intolerance; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Exercise tolerance; Dose adjustment: Reduce bisoprolol by 2.5mg increments; Alternative: Ranolazine; Patient warning: /Management: Gradual activity increase.
- BISOPROLOL + CAPTOPRIL; Risk: Additive cardiac depression; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: BP, HR, and signs of heart failure; Dose adjustment: Reduce bisoprolol by 25% if needed; Alternative: ARB monotherapy; Patient warning: /Management: Monitor for edema or dyspnea.
- BISOPROLOL + ENALAPRIL; Risk: Additive cardiac depression; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: BP, HR, and signs of HF; Dose adjustment: Reduce bisoprolol by 25% if needed; Alternative: ARB monotherapy; Patient warning: /Management: Monitor for edema.
- BISOPROLOL + LISINOPRIL; Risk: Cardiac depression; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: BP, HR, HF signs; Dose adjustment: Reduce bisoprolol by 25% if needed; Alternative: ARB monotherapy; Patient warning: /Management: Monitor for edema.
