1. What Daflon 1000 is used for
Chronic venous insufficiency (CVI) - 500 : 1,000 mg/day MPFF (diosmin + hesperidin) for 2 : 6 months*
Hemorrhoids - Acute attacks : 1,350 mg + 150 mg hesperidin × 4 days, then 900 mg + 100 mg × 3 days*
Venous leg ulcers - 900 mg + 100 mg hesperidin daily + compression therapy*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 1 tablet/day
Maximum daily dose: 3 tablets/day (3 tablets)
