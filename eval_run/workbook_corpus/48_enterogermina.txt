 Mix powder with water/juice or take capsules with meals.

 Keep below 25°C (spores degrade >30°C).
• Antibiotics:
 Take 2h apart from antibiotics.
• Report:
 Fever, bloody stools, or rash.
• Pregnancy:
 Safe at recommended doses.
