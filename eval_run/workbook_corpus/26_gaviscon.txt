1. What Gaviscon Advance is used for
Symptomatic Relief of Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD) and Heartburn - Particularly effective for "water brash" and regurgitation; Mechanism: Forms a gel like "raft" that floats on top of stomach contents, acting as a physical barrier to prevent reflux. Dosing (OTC): Typically taken after meals and at bedtime , Usual dose is 10 to 20 mL of liquid or 1 to 2 chewable tablets. Max frequency is usually 4 times daily. Always follow specific product label instructions*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: • None.
• Sodium Alginate, as an OTC agent, does not carry a Black Box Warning.
Warnings: • Dose Modification:
- Renal Impairment:
 Use with caution in severe impairment due to sodium content (in formulations containing sodium bicarbonate). Monitoring of fluid balance and blood pressure may be necessary.
- Hepatic Impairment:
 Use with caution in conditions predisposing to fluid overload (e.g., cirrhosis).
- Concomitant Therapy:
 Can interfere with the absorption of other oral medications. It is recommended to take other medications at least 2 hours before or after taking sodium alginate. This is due to its physical barrier-forming mechanism in the stomach.

• Medical Info for HCPs:
- Key Risks:
Patient education: • Best Time to Take:
- Take after meals and at bedtime.
- This ensures the protective raft is present when the stomach is full and when lying down, which can trigger reflux.
• How to Take:
- Chew tablets thoroughly before swallowing. Do not swallow them whole. For liquid, shake the bottle well and take the liquid undiluted.
• Missed Dose:
- Take it as soon as you remember.
- If it is almost time for your next dose, skip the missed dose. Do not double the dose.
• Interactions:
- Take other medications at least 2 hours before or after you take sodium alginate.
- It can block the absorption of other drugs.
• What to Watch For:
- If symptoms do not improve after 2 weeks, see your doctor. Stop us
