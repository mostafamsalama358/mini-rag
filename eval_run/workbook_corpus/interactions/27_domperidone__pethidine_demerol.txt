Clinical note:
Avoid combining domperidone with meperidine when possible due to additive risk of QT prolongation. Meperidine may reduce domperidone metabolism, raising its plasma concentration. Monitor ECG and neurologic status. Instruct patients to report any palpitations, dizziness, or syncope.
