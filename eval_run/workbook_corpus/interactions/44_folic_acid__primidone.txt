Clinical note:
Risk: ↓ Primidone/phenobarbital levels → increased seizure risk. Monitor: Phenobarbital levels (from primidone metabolism), seizure frequency. Dose Adjust: ↑ Primidone by 25% if phenobarbital <15 mcg/mL. Patient: Watch for tremor/nystagmus (toxicity when folate stopped).
