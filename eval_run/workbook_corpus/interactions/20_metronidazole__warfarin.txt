Drug interaction — Flagyl
Corpus type: RAG_CORPUS workbook / INTERACTIONS sheet
SKU ID: 20
Brand: Flagyl
API 1: METRONIDAZOLE
API 2: WARFARIN
Risk: Severe INR elevation (CYP2C9 inhibition)
Type: Pharmacokinetic
Monitoring: INR every 2-3 days during and 2 weeks post-therapy
Dose adjustment: Reduce warfarin by 30-50% preemptively
Alternative: Amoxicillin
Patient warning: Avoid alcohol (disulfiram-like reaction), report dark urine/bleeding.
Severity: yes
