1. What Ventolin is used for
Treatment and prevention of bronchospasm in patients with reversible obstructive airway disease - (e.g., asthma, exercise-induced bronchospasm); Dosing (MDI): Acute Bronchospasm: 2 puffs every 4 to 6 hours as needed , Exercise Induced: 2 puffs 15 to 30 minutes before exercise , Dosing (Nebulization): Adults & Children ≥12y: 2.5 mg 3 to 4 times daily as needed*
Prevention of exercise induced bronchospasm - Dosing (MDI): Acute Bronchospasm: 2 puffs every 4 to 6 hours as needed , Exercise Induced: 2 puffs 15 to 30 minutes before exercise , Dosing (Nebulization): Adults & Children ≥12y: 2.5 mg 3 to 4 times daily as needed*

Patient education: • Timing:
- Use only as needed for symptom relief or before exercise to prevent symptoms.
- Do not exceed the recommended dose.
• Technique:
- Use a spacer with your inhaler (MDI). Shake the inhaler well before each puff.
- Rinse your mouth with water after use to prevent thrush.
• Interactions:
- Inform your doctor if you use other asthma medicines, beta-blockers, or diuretics. Avoid caffeine and other stimulants.
• Missed Dose:
- This is a rescue inhaler. Use it when you have symptoms.
- You do not need to use it on a schedule if you have no symptoms.
• Overdose:
- Overuse can be dangerous.

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- SALBUTAMOL + METHYLDOPA; Risk: Paradoxical hypertension → stroke; Type: Antagonistic (β₂-agonist vs α₂-agonist); Monitoring: BP pre/post-inhalation, potassium; Dose adjustment: Increase methyldopa by 250mg/day; Alternatives: Ipratropium; Patient warning: "Report tremors/BP↑, avoid OTC decongestants"
- SALBUTAMOL + CLONIDINE HYDROCHLORIDE; Risk: Paradoxical hypertension → stroke; Type: Antagonistic (β₂-agonist vs α₂-agonist); Monitoring: BP pre/post-inhalation, ECG if tachycardic; Dose adjustment: Increase clonidine by 0.1mg/day; Alternatives: Ipratropium; Patient warning: "Report tremors/BP↑, avoid OTC decongestants"
- SALBUTAMOL + MOXONIDINE; Risk: Reduced antihypertensive efficacy; Type: Antagonistic; Monitoring: BP pre/post-inhalation; Dose adjustment: Increase moxonidine 0.2mg/day; Alternatives: Cromolyn; Patient warning: "Report tremors/BP↑"
- SALBUTAMOL + DIAZOXIDE; Risk: Severe hypotension and reflex tachycardia (potent arteriolar vasodilation + β₂-agonist effects); Type: Pharmacodynamic (additive cardiovascular effects); Monitoring: Continuous BP, ECG, blood glucose; Dose adjustment: Lower salbutamol dose/frequency; Alternative: Ipratropium for bronchospasm; Patient warning: …
- SALBUTAMOL + BOSENTAN; Risk: Additive hypotension and reflex tachycardia (bosentan vasodilation + β₂-agonist effects); Type: Pharmacodynamic (synergistic cardiovascular effects); Monitoring: BP, HR, oxygen saturation; Dose adjustment: Consider lower salbutamol dose/frequency; Alternative: Ipratropium bromide for bronchospasm

10. Possible side effects
Major: Nervousness - in children aged 2 to 6 years (>10%)*
Insomnia - in children aged 6 to 12 years receiving 4 to 12 mg q12hr (>10%)*
Tachycardia (>10%)*
Tremor - especially hand tremor (>10%)*
Headache (>10%)*
Minor: Palpitations (1-5%)*
Muscle cramps (1-10%)*
Dizziness (1-5%)*
Irritability (1-5%)*
Dry mouth (1-7%)*
Hypokalemia - especially with high doses (1-3%)*
Paradoxical Bronchospasm - wheezing immediately after use; stop drug immediately (1-2%)*
