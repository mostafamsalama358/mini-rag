1. What Buscopan is used for
Motion Sickness - Transdermal patch (1 mg/72h) or tablets (0.3 : 0.6 mg PRN)*
Postoperative Nausea/Vomiting (PONV) - Patch applied pre surgery*
Palliative Care - 0.2 : 0.4 mg SC q4h for excessive secretions*

2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 1-2 tablets 3-5 times/day
Maximum daily dose: 5 tablets/day (5 tablets)

3. Dosage — Children
Minimum age in catalog: 6 years
Pediatric min age: 6 years
Pediatric dose: 6-12 years: 10 mg three times daily.
Pediatric max daily dose: See pack

4. Do not take Buscopan if / disease cautions

Angle‑closure glaucoma*
Obstructive GI Diseases*
