2. Dosage — Adults
Adult dose: 1 tablet every 12 hours
Maximum daily dose: 2 tablets/day (2 tablets)

3. Dosage — Children

4. Do not take Hi-Biotic if / disease cautions
Hypersensitivity to β lactams * Clostridioides difficile-associated diarrhea (CDAD) * Infectious Mononucleosis * Infants under 3 months *

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings: • None
Warnings: • Cinnarizine interferes with the allergy test and gives a false result because there is no allergy to penicillins despite the presence of allergy. The solution, as mentioned in the scientific sources, is to stop the Cinnarizine drug 4 days before the allergy test 
• he must have a probiotic such as lacteol fort cap for use after it and advise him to eat prebiotic-rich food such as garlic, onions, bananas, yogurt
• Avoid taking too many calcium-enriched foods and drinks as it might affect the working of AMOXICILLIN.
• Avoid bacteriostatic agents (e.g., tetracyclines) which may antagonize effects
• Take with food.

6. Interactions (from workbook INTERACTIONS sheet)
- AMOXICILLIN + METHOTREXATE; Risk: Competitive tubular secretion → 60% ↑ MTX AUC.; Monitoring: Pre/post-dose creatinine clearance. Dose Adjustment: MTX ≤15mg/week + IV hydration.; Patient warning: Report ↓ urine output immediately.
- AMOXICILLIN + WARFARIN; Risk: Minimal INR change (occasional GI flora effect); Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: INR baseline + weekly; Dose adjustment: None typically; Alternative: Cephalexin; Patient warning: Report rash (mononucleosis contraindication).
- AMOXICILLIN + DICUMAROL; Risk: Minimal INR change; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: INR baseline + weekly; Dose adjustment: None; Alternative: Cephalexin; Patient warning: Maintain consistent vitamin K intake.
- AMOXICILLIN + PHENPROCOUMON; Risk: Minimal INR change; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: INR baseline + weekly; Dose adjustment: None; Alternative: Cephalexin; Patient warning: Maintain consistent vitamin K intake.
- AMOXICILLIN + ACENOCOUMAROL; Risk: Minimal INR change; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: INR baseline + weekly; Dose adjustment: None; Alternative: Cephalexin; Patient warning: Maintain consistent vitamin K intake.
