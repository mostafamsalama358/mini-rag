NORTHBRIDGE CLINICAL NOTE — NSAID Use in Breastfeeding (E2E Test Corpus)
Document ID: NB-LACT-IBU-E2E
Audience: Pharmacists and clinicians

Ibuprofen is generally considered compatible with breastfeeding when used at standard short-term doses.
MediQuick Ibuprofen 200 mg may be used by breastfeeding mothers if clinically appropriate.
Prefer the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration.
Advise the mother to watch the infant for unusual sleepiness, feeding problems, or rash and to seek advice if these occur.

Prefer ibuprofen over longer-acting NSAIDs when an NSAID is required during lactation.
Paracetamol remains a first-line analgesic option in breastfeeding when pain relief is needed and NSAIDs are unsuitable.
