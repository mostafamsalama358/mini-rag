GLUCOPHAGE Metformin 500 mg Tablets
Patient Information Leaflet — E2E Pharmacy Corpus
Product code: GP-MET-500-E2E

1. What Glucophage is used for
Glucophage (metformin) is used for type 2 diabetes, usually with diet and exercise. It lowers blood glucose and may help insulin sensitivity.

2. Dosage
Adults: often start 500 mg once or twice daily with meals, titrated by the clinician.
Maximum daily dose is commonly 2000–2550 mg depending on product/label — follow the prescribed regimen.
Swallow with water during or after food to reduce stomach upset.

3. Do not take if
- You have severe kidney impairment (per local eGFR cut-offs).
- You have acute metabolic acidosis / lactic acidosis risk states as advised by your doctor.
- You are allergic to metformin.

4. Warnings
Stop and seek urgent care for unexplained vomiting, deep rapid breathing, severe tiredness, or sudden kidney illness.
Hold metformin around iodinated contrast procedures as instructed by the hospital/clinic.

5. Interactions
Alcohol binge increases lactic acidosis risk.
Some heart/kidney medicines need renal monitoring with metformin.
Contrast media: follow hold/restart protocol.

6. Supplements
Long-term therapy: consider vitamin B12 monitoring; supplement if deficient.
Do not replace metformin with herbal “glucose support” products.

7. Side effects
Common: nausea, diarrhoea, abdominal discomfort (often improve with slow titration and food).
Serious (rare): lactic acidosis — emergency.

8. Storage
Store below 25°C.
