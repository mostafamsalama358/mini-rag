LIPITOR Atorvastatin 20 mg Tablets
Patient Information Leaflet — E2E Pharmacy Corpus
Product code: LP-ATOR-20-E2E

1. What Lipitor is used for
Lipitor (atorvastatin) lowers LDL cholesterol and is used to reduce cardiovascular risk as part of lifestyle + medicine plans.

2. Dosage
Adults: commonly 10–20 mg once daily, adjusted by clinician up to higher strengths when needed.
Take at the same time each day, with or without food.
Do not take more than prescribed.

3. Do not take if
- Active liver disease or unexplained persistent raised liver enzymes.
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Allergy to atorvastatin.

4. Interactions
Avoid combining with gemfibrozil.
Macrolide antibiotics (clarithromycin/erythromycin) and some azole antifungals increase myopathy risk.
Limit large amounts of grapefruit juice.
Tell your pharmacist if you take warfarin, amlodipine, or immunosuppressants.

5. Warnings
Report unexplained muscle pain, tenderness, weakness, or dark urine promptly.
Liver tests may be monitored.

6. Supplements
Avoid red yeast rice with atorvastatin.
CoQ10 is optional for mild muscle complaints only after clinical review — not for severe myopathy.
Correct vitamin D deficiency if present; it does not replace safety monitoring.

7. Side effects
Common: headache, nausea, diarrhoea, joint pains.
Serious: myopathy/rhabdomyolysis, liver injury (rare).

8. Storage
Store below 25°C.
