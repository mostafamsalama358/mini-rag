CATAFLAM Diclofenac Potassium 50 mg Tablets
Short Product Information — E2E Evaluation Fixture
Product code: CF-DIC-50-E2E

1. Indications
Cataflam is used for acute pain and inflammation, including post-traumatic pain, postoperative pain, and acute flares of osteoarthritis.

2. Dosage
Adults: 50 mg two to three times daily.
Maximum daily dose: 150 mg.
Take with food if stomach upset occurs.

3. Contraindications
Do not take Cataflam if you have active peptic ulcer, gastrointestinal bleeding, severe heart failure, or known hypersensitivity to diclofenac or other NSAIDs.
Do not use in the third trimester of pregnancy.

4. Breastfeeding
Diclofenac passes into breast milk in small amounts. Short-term use may be acceptable; avoid prolonged high-dose therapy while breastfeeding.

5. Storage
Store below 25°C. Protect from moisture.
