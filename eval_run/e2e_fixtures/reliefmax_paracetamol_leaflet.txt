RELIEFMAX Paracetamol 500 mg Tablets
Patient Information Leaflet — Test Document for E2E Evaluation
Product code: RM-PARA-500-E2E
Manufacturer: Northbridge Pharma Labs

1. What ReliefMax Paracetamol is used for
ReliefMax Paracetamol is used for the relief of mild to moderate pain such as headache, toothache, and muscular aches. It also reduces fever.

2. Dosage — Adults and adolescents (16 years and over)
Take one or two tablets (500 mg to 1000 mg) with water every 4 to 6 hours as needed.
Do not take more than 8 tablets (4000 mg) in any 24-hour period.
Leave at least 4 hours between doses.
Do not use for more than 3 days without medical advice.

3. Dosage — Children
ReliefMax Paracetamol 500 mg tablets are not recommended for children under 16 years of age.
Ask a doctor or pharmacist for an age-appropriate liquid formulation.

4. Do not take ReliefMax Paracetamol if
- You are allergic to paracetamol or any of the tablet excipients.
- You have severe liver disease.
- You are already taking other medicines that contain paracetamol.

5. Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Paracetamol may be used during pregnancy if clearly needed and at the lowest effective dose for the shortest time.
ReliefMax Paracetamol can be used while breastfeeding at recommended doses; only small amounts pass into breast milk.

6. Warnings and precautions
Talk to your doctor before use if you have mild to moderate liver or kidney problems, or if you regularly drink large amounts of alcohol.
Taking more than the recommended dose can cause serious liver damage.
Seek urgent medical help if you think you have taken too much, even if you feel well.

7. Interactions
Do not take with other paracetamol-containing products.
Use with caution with warfarin during prolonged regular use — INR monitoring may be needed.
Metoclopramide and domperidone may increase absorption of paracetamol.

8. Possible side effects
Common: none at recommended doses for most people.
Rare: skin rash, allergic reaction.
Very rare but serious: severe skin reactions, blood disorders, liver damage with overdose.

9. Storage
Store below 30°C in a dry place.
Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
Do not use after the expiry date printed on the blister and carton.

10. Ingredients
Each tablet contains paracetamol 500 mg.
Excipients include maize starch, povidone, and magnesium stearate.
