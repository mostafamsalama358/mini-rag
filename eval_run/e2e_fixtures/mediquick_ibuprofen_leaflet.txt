1. What MediQuick Ibuprofen is used for
MediQuick Ibuprofen is used for the short-term relief of mild to moderate pain such as headache, dental pain, period pain, and backache. It also reduces fever and inflammation associated with colds and flu.

2. Dosage — Adults and adolescents (12 years and over)
Take one or two tablets (200 mg to 400 mg) with water every 6 to 8 hours as needed.
Do not take more than 6 tablets (1200 mg) in any 24-hour period.
Leave at least 6 hours between doses.
Do not use for more than 3 days for fever or 5 days for pain without medical advice.

3. Dosage — Children under 12 years
MediQuick Ibuprofen 200 mg tablets are not recommended for children under 12 years of age.
Ask a doctor or pharmacist for an age-appropriate formulation.

4. Do not take MediQuick Ibuprofen if
- You are allergic to ibuprofen, aspirin, or any other NSAID.
- You have an active peptic ulcer or a history of gastrointestinal bleeding related to NSAIDs.
- You are in the last 3 months of pregnancy (third trimester).
- You have severe heart failure, severe kidney failure, or severe liver failure.

5. Warnings and precautions
Talk to your doctor before use if you have asthma, high blood pressure, mild to moderate kidney or liver problems, or if you are aged 65 or over.
Long-term use of high doses may increase the risk of heart attack or stroke.
Stop taking and seek medical help if you develop a rash, facial swelling, wheezing, black stools, or vomiting blood.

6. Interactions
Do not take with other NSAIDs (including aspirin at pain-relief doses) unless advised by a doctor.
Use with caution with warfarin or other anticoagulants — bleeding risk may increase.
Combining with SSRIs may also increase gastrointestinal bleeding risk.

7. Possible side effects
Common: indigestion, heartburn, nausea, stomach pain.
Uncommon: headache, dizziness, skin rash.
Rare but serious: stomach ulcer, gastrointestinal bleeding, severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis), kidney problems.

8. Storage
Store below 25°C in the original blister pack.
Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
Do not use after the expiry date printed on the carton.

9. Ingredients
Each tablet contains ibuprofen 200 mg.
Excipients include microcrystalline cellulose, croscarmellose sodium, and magnesium stearate.
