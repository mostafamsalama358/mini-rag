LOSEC Omeprazole 20 mg Capsules
Patient Information Leaflet — E2E Pharmacy Corpus
Product code: LS-OME-20-E2E

1. What Losec is used for
Losec (omeprazole) reduces stomach acid. It is used for reflux (heartburn), stomach ulcers, and prevention of NSAID-related ulcers when prescribed.

2. Dosage
Adults (reflux): usually 20 mg once daily in the morning before food for 2 to 4 weeks, or as prescribed.
Do not crush/chew delayed-release capsules.
Do not take more than prescribed.

3. Contraindications and cautions
Do not take if allergic to omeprazole or other PPIs.
Seek advice before use in severe liver disease.
Long-term use needs clinical review (magnesium, B12, fracture risk counselling).

4. Interactions
May interact with clopidogrel, methotrexate, digoxin, and some antifungals/antivirals.
Tell your pharmacist about warfarin, HIV medicines, and epilepsy medicines.
Iron and some calcium supplements may be less well absorbed — ask about timing.

5. Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Use only if clearly needed and advised by a clinician.

6. Side effects
Common: headache, diarrhoea, stomach pain, nausea.
Uncommon: dizziness, rash.
Seek help for severe diarrhoea, rash with blistering, or muscle cramps (possible low magnesium).

7. Storage
Store below 25°C. Keep dry and out of children's reach.
