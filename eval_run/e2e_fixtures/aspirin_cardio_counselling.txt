NORTHBRIDGE CLINICAL NOTE — Low-Dose Aspirin Counselling (E2E Corpus)
Document ID: NB-ASA-CARDIO-E2E

Low-dose aspirin (commonly 75–100 mg daily) is used for antiplatelet protection when prescribed.

Interactions and cautions:
Aspirin + warfarin: higher bleeding risk; only under specialist/clinician plans, often with gastroprotection.
Aspirin + ibuprofen: regular ibuprofen may reduce aspirin’s antiplatelet effect if taken immediately beforehand; prefer paracetamol for pain when on cardio aspirin, or separate timing per pharmacist advice.
Aspirin + SSRIs: increased GI bleed risk.
Aspirin + alcohol binge: higher GI bleed risk.

Supplements:
High-dose fish oil, ginkgo, or garlic tablets may add bleed risk with aspirin — review before starting.
Avoid starting multiple “heart health” herbals without pharmacist check.

What to take for pain while on aspirin:
Prefer paracetamol at standard doses.
Avoid OTC ibuprofen/naproxen/diclofenac unless a clinician agrees.
