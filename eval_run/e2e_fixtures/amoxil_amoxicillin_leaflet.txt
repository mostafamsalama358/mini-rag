AMOXIL Amoxicillin 500 mg Capsules
Patient Information Leaflet — E2E Pharmacy Corpus
Product code: AX-AMOX-500-E2E

1. What Amoxil is used for
Amoxil (amoxicillin) is a penicillin antibiotic used for bacterial infections of the ear, nose, throat, chest, skin, and urinary tract when caused by susceptible organisms.

2. Dosage — Adults
Take one 500 mg capsule three times daily (every 8 hours), or as directed by your doctor.
Usual course: 5 to 7 days. Complete the full course even if you feel better.
Do not exceed the prescribed dose.

3. Dosage — Children
Paediatric dosing is weight-based. Do not use adult 500 mg capsules for young children unless advised by a clinician.
Ask a pharmacist for an age-appropriate suspension.

4. Do not take Amoxil if
- You are allergic to amoxicillin, penicillin, or any other beta-lactam antibiotic.
- You have had a severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) to any antibiotic before.

5. Warnings and precautions
Tell your doctor if you have kidney problems, glandular fever (mononucleosis), or a history of antibiotic-associated colitis.
Stop and seek help if you develop a severe rash, facial swelling, wheezing, or watery/bloody diarrhoea.

6. Interactions
Amoxicillin may reduce the effectiveness of combined oral contraceptives — use additional contraception during the course and for 7 days after.
Probenecid increases amoxicillin levels.
Methotrexate toxicity risk may increase — monitor closely.
Warfarin: occasional INR changes reported; monitor INR if used together.
Do not combine with bacteriostatic antibiotics (e.g. tetracyclines) unless advised.

7. Supplements and food
Amoxicillin can be taken with or without food.
Probiotic supplements (e.g. Saccharomyces boulardii or Lactobacillus) may be used during/after the course to support gut flora; separate from the antibiotic dose by at least 2 hours.
Avoid taking calcium-heavy supplements or antacids in the same hour if using certain combinations — for Amoxil alone, standard meals are fine.
Iron supplements: separate by 2 hours when possible to reduce stomach upset.

8. Possible side effects
Common: diarrhoea, nausea, rash.
Uncommon: vomiting, thrush.
Rare but serious: severe allergic reaction, antibiotic-associated colitis.

9. Storage
Store below 25°C in the original blister. Keep out of reach of children.
