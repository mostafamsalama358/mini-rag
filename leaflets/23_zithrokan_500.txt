7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Macrolides used when indicated; azithromycin often acceptable with clinician oversight.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Usually compatible short courses; monitor infant GI flora.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
