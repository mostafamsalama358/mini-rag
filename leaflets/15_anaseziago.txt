1. What Anaseziago is used for
Catalog uses: local anesthetic
Approved uses (material sheet): Local anesthesia - 0.5 to 2% solutions*
Ventricular tachycardia - 1 to 1.5 mg/kg IV bolus*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Neuropathic pain - 5% patches (max 3 patches/day)*

2. Dosage — Adults
Web-supplemented adult dosing: Intact skin minor procedures: apply thick layer under occlusive dressing, typically ~1.5 g/10 cm² for ~1 hour (follow pack). Do not apply to broken skin/eyes/mucosa unless directed.
Catalog DOSE field may still be empty — prefer this section + official Egyptian pack.

3. Dosage — Children
Web-supplemented paediatric dosing: Strict max dose/area/time by age & weight (e.g. 0–3 mo ≤1 g/10 cm²/1 h; older children higher per EMLA table). Avoid in <37 weeks gestation neonates; caution with methemoglobin-inducing drugs in infants <12 mo.

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Diazoxide/Lidocaine + RALOXIFENE HYDROCHLORIDE: Risk: Raloxifene's plasma protein binding displacement increases free fractions of both drugs (diazoxide ↑40%, lidocaine ↑35%). Effects: Severe hypotension (SBP <90mmHg), neurotoxicity (lidocaine levels >5mcg/mL), and hyperglycemia (glucose >250mg/dL). Monitoring: Continuous ECG (QTc), BP q5min, glucose checks. Dosage…
Also avoid duplicating the same actives in other cold/pain/antibiotic products.

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Former FDA Cat B; use only if clearly needed (limited human data).

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Small milk amounts; EMC states can be used if clinically needed at therapeutic topical doses.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
