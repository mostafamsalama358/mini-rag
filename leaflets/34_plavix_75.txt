1. What Plavix is used for
Catalog uses: antiplatlet.adp receptor blocker
Approved uses (material sheet): Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS) - Unstable Angina (UA), Non ST Elevation MI (NSTEMI), ST Elevation MI (STEMI) - 300 mg or 600 mg loading dose, then 75 mg once daily*
Recent MI, Recent Stroke, or Established Peripheral Arterial Disease (PAD) - 75 mg once daily*
Post Percutaneous Coronary Intervention (PCI) with stent placement - 75 mg once daily with Aspirin 81 mg daily. Duration depends on indication and stent type (e.g., 1 : 12 months or longer)*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Atrial Fibrillation - ( when Warfarin/DOACs are contraindicated), often with Aspirin*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): WARNING: DIMINISHED EFFECTIVENESS IN POOR METABOLIZERS

Effectiveness of Clopidogrel depends on activation by CYP2C19.

• Patients who are poor metabolizers (2-14% of population) receive less antiplatelet effect.
• Tests are available to identify a patient's CYP2C19 genotype.
• Consider alternative treatment strategies in patients identified as poor metabolizers.
Notes (material sheet): • Take with or without food
• Avoid eating grapefruit or drinking grapefruit juice while using this medication
• PROTON PUMP INHIBITORS: USE WITH CAUTION. Avoid concomitant use with Omeprazole or Esomeprazole. Rationale: They inhibit CYP2C19, significantly reducing Clopidogrel's efficacy. Pantoprazole or H2 blockers are preferred
• Vitamin K: AVOID high-dose supplements. Does not directly interact, but can counteract the anti-thrombotic state.
