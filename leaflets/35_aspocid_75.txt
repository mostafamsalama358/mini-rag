5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): • Risk of serious GI bleeding (especially in elderly)
• Reye's syndrome in children with viral infections
Notes (material sheet): • Poor absorption of the drug (Aspirin) in the body, and the reason is that some heart patients use enteric-coated aspirin instead of uncoated aspirin to avoid stomach ulcers
• Avoid alcohol. Alcohol increases the risk of gastrointestinal bleeding.
• Avoid herbs and supplements with anticoagulant/antiplatelet activity. Examples include garlic, ginger, bilberry, danshen, piracetam, and ginkgo biloba.May increase bleeding risk
• Take after a meal. This reduces irritating gastrointestinal effects.
• Take with a full glass of water.
• Do not lie down for at least 10 minutes after you have taken this drug. If stomach upset occurs while you are taking this medication, you may take it with food or…
Patient education excerpt: • Timing: 
-Take with food/milk to reduce GI upset
• Missed dose: 
-Skip if close to next dose (for chronic use)
• Warning: 
-Report black stools, ringing ears, or swelling
• Antidote: 
-Vitamin K/FFP for major bleeding

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Low-dose aspirin used in obstetric protocols when indicated; avoid analgesic-dose NSAID aspirin especially late pregnancy.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Low-dose often acceptable; avoid high-dose.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
