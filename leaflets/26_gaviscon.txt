1. What Gaviscon Advance is used for
Catalog uses: antacid
Approved uses (material sheet): Symptomatic Relief of Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD) and Heartburn - Particularly effective for "water brash" and regurgitation; Mechanism: Forms a gel like "raft" that floats on top of stomach contents, acting as a physical barrier to prevent reflux. Dosing (OTC): Typically taken after meals and at bedtime , Usual dose is 10 to 20 mL of liquid or 1 to 2 chewable tablets. Max frequency is usually 4 times daily. Always follow specific product label instructions*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Laryngopharyngeal Reflux (LPR/Silent Reflux)*
As a mechanical barrier to protect the esophagus - in patients with persistent reflux*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): • None.
• Sodium Alginate, as an OTC agent, does not carry a Black Box Warning.
Notes (material sheet): • Dose Modification:
- Renal Impairment:
 Use with caution in severe impairment due to sodium content (in formulations containing sodium bicarbonate). Monitoring of fluid balance and blood pressure may be necessary.
- Hepatic Impairment:
 Use with caution in conditions predisposing to fluid overload (e.g., cirrhosis).
- Concomitant Therapy:
 Can interfere with the absorption of other oral medications. It is recommended to take other medications at least 2 hours before or after taking sodium alginate. This is due to its physical barrier-forming mechanism in the stomach.

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Sodium Alginate + CAPTOPRIL: Risk: Minimal absorption impact; Type: Minimal interaction; Monitoring: Routine BP monitoring; Dosage Adjustments: None typically needed; Alternative: Preferred for GERD with ACEIs; Patient Warnings/Management: Can be taken together if needed.
- Sodium Alginate + BENAZEPRIL: Risk: No clinically relevant interaction; Type: Non-interacting; Monitoring: Standard care; Dosage Adjustments: None; Alternative: Preferred for GERD; Patient Warnings/Management: Safe for any administration timing.
- Sodium Alginate + CILAZAPRIL: Risk: No interaction expected; Type: Non-interacting; Monitoring: Standard BP monitoring; Dosage Adjustments: None; Alternative: Preferred for GERD; Patient Warnings/Management: No administration restrictions.
- Sodium Alginate + ALUMINUM ASPIRIN: Risk: Mild absorption delay; Type: Physical barrier effect; Monitoring: Clinical response; Dosage Adjustments: Usually not required; Alternative: Alginate-free formulations; Patient Warnings/Management: Take aspirin first if GERD symptoms.
- Sodium Alginate + ACETYL SALICYLIC ACID: Risk: Minimal clinical impact; Type: Mechanical interference; Monitoring: Not typically needed; Dosage Adjustments: None; Alternative: Non-alginate antacids; Patient Warnings/Management: Normal dosing acceptable.
- Sodium Alginate + ASPIRIN: Risk: Minimal absorption impact; Type: Mechanical barrier effect; Monitoring: Not typically required; Dosage Adjustments: None; Alternative: Alginate-free antireflux agents; Patient Warnings/Management: Take aspirin first if GERD symptoms.
- Sodium Alginate + DIPYRIDAMOLE: Risk: Mucosal barrier interference. Type: Physical. Monitoring: GERD symptom control. Dosage Adjustments: Liquid formulation spacing. Alternative: H2 blockers. Patient Warnings/Management: Upright positioning after doses.
- Sodium Alginate + DIGOXIN: Risk: Minimal interaction (physical barrier mechanism); Type: Low-risk; Monitoring: Routine monitoring; Dosage Adjustments: None typically; Alternative: Administer digoxin 1h before alginate; Patient Warnings/Management: No special precautions, Avoid high-fiber meals (reduces digoxin absorption)
- Sodium Alginate + GEMFIBROZIL: Risk: Minimal clinical interaction. Type: Pharmacokinetic (negligible). Monitoring: Routine lipid monitoring. Dosage Adjustments: None required. Alternative: Alginate-antacid combinations. Patient Warnings/Management: Standard dosing acceptable.
- Sodium Alginate + FENOFIBRATE: Risk: No interaction expected. Type: Pharmacokinetic (none). Monitoring: Routine care. Dosage Adjustments: Unnecessary. Alternative: No restrictions. Patient Warnings/Management: No special precautions.
- Sodium Alginate + CLOFIBRATE: Risk: No significant interaction. Type: Pharmacokinetic (none). Monitoring: Routine lipid tests. Dosage Adjustments: Unnecessary. Alternative: Alginate combinations acceptable. Patient Warnings/Management: No special precautions needed.
- Sodium Alginate + ATORVASTATIN: Risk: No significant interaction. Type: Pharmacokinetic. Monitoring: Routine lipid tests. Dosage Adjustments: Unnecessary. Alternative: Alginate combinations acceptable. Patient Warnings/Management: No special precautions.
