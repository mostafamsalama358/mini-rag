1. What Daflon 500 is used for
Catalog uses: vascular-protecting agent
Approved uses (material sheet): Chronic venous insufficiency (CVI) - 500 : 1,000 mg/day MPFF (diosmin + hesperidin) for 2 : 6 months*
Hemorrhoids - Acute attacks : 1,350 mg + 150 mg hesperidin × 4 days, then 900 mg + 100 mg × 3 days*
Venous leg ulcers - 900 mg + 100 mg hesperidin daily + compression therapy*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Varicocele/Pelvic congestion - 1,000 mg/day × 6 months 67*
Post CABG recovery - 3,000 mg/day preoperatively*

2. Dosage — Adults
Web-supplemented adult dosing: Venous insufficiency: 2 tablets daily (1 midday + 1 evening) with meals. Acute hemorrhoids: 6 tablets/day for 4 days then 4 tablets/day for 3 days (Servier SmPC).
Catalog DOSE field may still be empty — prefer this section + official Egyptian pack.

3. Dosage — Children
Web-supplemented paediatric dosing: Not established under 18 years (Servier SmPC).

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Prefer avoid as precaution — limited human data; animal studies not teratogenic (Servier).

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Not recommended — unknown if excreted in milk (Servier).

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
