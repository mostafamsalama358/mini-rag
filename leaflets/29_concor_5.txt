1. What Concor is used for
Catalog uses: anti-hypertensive.beta blocker.selective b1
Approved uses (material sheet): Hypertension - Initial dose 5 mg once daily. May be increased to 10 mg, and then up to 20 mg once daily if needed*
Chronic Heart Failure (NYHA Class II , III) - Must be initiated at a very low dose and titrated slowly. Initial: 1.25 mg once daily. Target: 10 mg once daily, if tolerated*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Stable Coronary Artery Disease / Angina Pectoris - 5 to 20 mg once daily*
Supraventricular Arrhythmias - For rate control*

2. Dosage — Adults

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Bisoprolol + AMIODARONE: Risk: ↑ Bisoprolol levels → exercise intolerance; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Exercise tolerance; Dosage Adjustments: Reduce bisoprolol by 2.5mg increments; Alternative: Ranolazine; Patient Warnings/Management: Gradual activity increase.
- Bisoprolol + CAPTOPRIL: Risk: Additive cardiac depression; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: BP, HR, and signs of heart failure; Dosage Adjustments: Reduce bisoprolol by 25% if needed; Alternative: ARB monotherapy; Patient Warnings/Management: Monitor for edema or dyspnea.
- Bisoprolol + ENALAPRIL: Risk: Additive cardiac depression; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: BP, HR, and signs of HF; Dosage Adjustments: Reduce bisoprolol by 25% if needed; Alternative: ARB monotherapy; Patient Warnings/Management: Monitor for edema.
- Bisoprolol + LISINOPRIL: Risk: Cardiac depression; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: BP, HR, HF signs; Dosage Adjustments: Reduce bisoprolol by 25% if needed; Alternative: ARB monotherapy; Patient Warnings/Management: Monitor for edema.
- Bisoprolol + RAMIPRIL: Risk: Cardiac depression; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: BP, HR, HF signs; Dosage Adjustments: Reduce bisoprolol by 25% if needed; Alternative: ARB monotherapy; Patient Warnings/Management: Monitor for edema.

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Beta-blockers may be used when indicated; fetal growth monitoring may be needed — specialist care.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Bisoprolol: limited data; monitor infant for bradycardia/hypoglycaemia if used.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
