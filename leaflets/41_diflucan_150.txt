1. What Diflucan is used for
Catalog uses: antifungals.triazoles
Approved uses (material sheet): Oropharyngeal Candidiasis - 200 mg day 1, then 100 mg daily × 7 : 14d*
Vaginal Candidiasis - 150 mg × 1 dose*
Cryptococcal Meningitis - 400 : 800 mg daily × 10 : 12w (induction)*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Candida UTI - 200 to 400 mg daily × 14d*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): • HEPATIC TOXICITY & QT PROLONGATION:
"May cause severe hepatotoxicity. Monitor LFTs. Avoid in congenital long QT syndrome or with other QT-prolonging drugs."
Notes (material sheet): • Take this medication by mouth usually as a single dose, with or without food at any time of the day
• All azole antifungal medicines: cause liver problems and cause heart rhythm disturbances because they prolong the QT except fluconazole, which is excreted through the kidneys
• It increases the level of INR in the patient who uses warfarin, and the most dangerous of them is fluconazole, ketoconazole, voriconazole, so it is necessary to monitor the INR level well.
• Avoid:
- St. John’s wort (↓ fluconazole levels via CYP3A4 induction).
- Alcohol (↑ hepatotoxicity risk)

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Fluconazole + AMIODARONE: Risk: Sudden cardiac death (QTc >550ms); Type: CYP3A4 + hERG inhibition; Monitoring: LFTs + QTc weekly; Dosage Adjustments: Contraindicated; Alternative: Isavuconazonium; Patient Warnings: Avoid grapefruit.
- Fluconazole + DRONEDARONE: Risk: QT prolongation + CYP3A4 inhibition; Type: Mixed; Monitoring: QTc if >400mg/day; Dosage Adjustments: Max fluconazole 200mg/day; Alternative: Anidulafungin; Patient Warnings/Management: Watch for dizziness.
- Fluconazole + PROPAFENONE: Risk: QT prolongation + CYP3A4 inhibition; Type: Mixed; Monitoring: QTc if >400mg/day; Dosage Adjustments: Max fluconazole 200mg/day; Alternative: Anidulafungin; Patient Warnings/Management: Watch for dizziness.
- Fluconazole + SILODOSIN: Risk: Moderate interaction (mild CYP3A4 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP biweekly; Dosage Adjustments: Max silodosin 4mg/day; Alternative: Preferred antifungal option; Patient Warnings/Management: Standard hypotension precautions.
- Fluconazole + TAMSULOSIN: Risk: Mild tamsulosin increase; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP weekly; Dosage Adjustments: None typically needed; Alternative: First-line antifungal option; Patient Warnings/Management: Normal activities permitted.
- Fluconazole + PRAZOSIN: Risk: Moderate hypotension (mild CYP3A4 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP daily x7; Dosage Adjustments: Start prazosin 0.5mg at bedtime; Alternative: Preferred antifungal option; Patient Warnings/Management: Rise slowly.
- Fluconazole + ALFUZOSIN: Risk: Mild QT prolongation risk (high-dose fluconazole); Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: ECG if >400mg/day fluconazole; Dosage Adjustments: None typically needed; Alternative: Terbinafine if cardiac risk; Patient Warnings/Management: Monitor for arrhythmias.
- Fluconazole + DOXAZOSIN: Risk: Moderate interaction (mild CYP3A4 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP biweekly; Dosage Adjustments: Max doxazosin 4mg/day; Alternative: Preferred antifungal option; Patient Warnings/Management: Standard hypotension precautions.
- Fluconazole + TERAZOSIN: Risk: Moderate hypotension (mild CYP3A4 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP daily x7; Dosage Adjustments: Start terazosin 1mg at bedtime; Alternative: Preferred antifungal option; Patient Warnings/Management: Rise slowly.
- Fluconazole + tolazoline: Risk: Moderate hypotension (mild CYP3A4 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP q15min during infusion; Dosage Adjustments: Reduce tolazoline infusion rate by 50%; Alternative: Preferred antifungal option; Patient Warnings/Management: Emergency BP protocol.
- Fluconazole + KETANSERIN: Risk: TdP (CYP2C9 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic + dynamic; Monitoring: ECG + ketanserin levels; Dosage Adjustments: Avoid >200mg/day fluconazole; Alternatives: Anidulafungin; Patient Warnings: "Report dizziness"
- Fluconazole + DIAZOXIDE: Risk: QTc prolongation (≥500 ms) + fluid retention; Type: Pharmacodynamic synergy; Monitoring: ECG weekly, daily weights, renal function; Dosage: Fluconazole ≤200 mg/day; Alternative: Anidulafungin; Patient Warnings: Report swelling/irregular heartbeat, limit salt intake .
Also avoid duplicating the same actives in other cold/pain/antibiotic products.

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Avoid single/high-dose fluconazole in pregnancy especially 1st trimester (teratogenicity signals at high/prolonged doses).

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Compatible after single 150 mg dose in many references; confirm for multi-day courses.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
