1. What Brufen is used for

Approved uses (material sheet): Pain/fever - 200 : 400mg q4 : 6h (max 1200mg/day OTC; 3200mg/day Rx)*
RA/OA - 400 : 800mg TID (max 2400 : 3200mg/day)*

2. Dosage — Adults
Web-supplemented adult dosing: 200–400 mg every 6–8 h with food; OTC max often 1200 mg/day; prescription regimens may go higher under supervision.

3. Dosage — Children

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Avoid in 3rd trimester; avoid if possible earlier unless advised.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Usually compatible in short courses at usual doses; prefer after feeds if concerned.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
