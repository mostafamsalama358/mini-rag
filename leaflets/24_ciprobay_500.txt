6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Ciprofloxacin + CLOPIDOGREL: Risk: Decreased clopidogrel activation (CYP2C19 inhibition). Type: Pharmacokinetic. Monitoring: Therapeutic efficacy (e.g., recurrent CV events). Dosage Adjustments: Avoid high-dose ciprofloxacin (>750mg BID). Alternative: Levofloxacin or non-fluoroquinolone antibiotics. Patient Warnings/Management: Monitor for treatm…
- Ciprofloxacin + DULOXETINE: Risk: ↑ Duloxetine levels (CYP1A2 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP, serotonin syndrome signs; Dosage Adjustment: Max duloxetine 40mg/day; Alternative: Levofloxacin; Patient Warning: Discontinue if hypertensive crisis occurs.
- Ciprofloxacin + PHENYTOIN: Risk: Ciprofloxacin ↑ phenytoin levels (moderate CYP2C9 inhibition). Monitoring: Phenytoin levels within 3 days. Dosage: ↓ phenytoin by 20-30% if combined >3 days. Patient Warnings: Watch for nausea or diplopia.
- Ciprofloxacin + ZOLMITRIPTAN: Risk: Possible reduced zolmitriptan efficacy; Monitoring: Document migraine attack duration/severity; Dosage: Adjust dose based on clinical response; Patient Warnings: Avoid missing doses due to interaction risk.
- Ciprofloxacin + ROPINIROLE: Risk: CYP1A2 inhibition ↑ ropinirole exposure → hallucinations, syncope, and impulse control disorders; Monitoring: Neuropsychiatric eval weekly, orthostatic BP; Dosage: Halve ropinirole dose if coadministered >3 days; Patient: Report gambling urges; rise slowly from sitting.
- Ciprofloxacin + CLOZAPINE: Risk: CYP1A2 inhibition → ↑ clozapine levels (seizure/sedation). Monitoring: Clozapine levels, EEG. Dose Adjust: Reduce clozapine by 40-60%. Patient Warning: Report myoclonic jerks; avoid driving.
- Ciprofloxacin + OLANZAPINE: Risk: CYP1A2 inhibition → ↑ olanzapine (metabolic toxicity). Monitoring: Glucose, lipids; olanzapine levels. Dose Adjust: Halve olanzapine dose. Patient Warning: Monitor weight/glucose; report excessive hunger.
- Ciprofloxacin + BUPROPION: Risk: Ciprofloxacin (CYP1A2 inhibitor) may modestly ↑ bupropion levels. Action: Limit bupropion to 300mg/day (450mg if essential). Monitoring: Seizure precautions, especially in elderly.
- Ciprofloxacin + METHOTREXATE: Risk: OATP1B1 inhibition → hepatic MTX retention. Monitoring: Daily LFTs +胆汁酸. Dose Adjustment: MTX ≤15mg/m² with cipro. Patient Warning: Pruritus precedes hepatotoxicity.
- Ciprofloxacin + WARFARIN: Risk: Significant INR increase (CYP1A2 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: INR every 2-3 days; Dosage: Reduce warfarin by 30-40%; Alternative: Levofloxacin; Patient Warnings: Hydrate well, report tendon pain.
- Ciprofloxacin + DICUMAROL: Risk: Marked INR increase (CYP1A2 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: INR every 2-3 days; Dosage: Reduce dicumarol by 35-45%; Alternative: Levofloxacin; Patient Warnings: Hydrate well, report tendon pain.
- Ciprofloxacin + PHENPROCOUMON: Risk: Marked INR increase (CYP1A2 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: INR every 2-3 days; Dosage: Reduce phenprocoumon by 35-45%; Alternative: Levofloxacin; Patient Warnings: Hydrate well, report tendon pain.
Also avoid duplicating the same actives in other cold/pain/antibiotic products.

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Avoid if safer alternatives exist (fluoroquinolone caution).

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Generally avoid; excreted in milk.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
