1. What Flagyl is used for
Catalog uses: antiprotozoal.nitroimidazole
Approved uses (material sheet): Anaerobic infections - 500 mg IV/PO q6 to 8h*
Amebiasis - 500 to 750 mg PO q8hr for 5 to 10 days & Pediatric: 35 to 50 mg/kg/day in 3 divided doses x 10 days*
C. difficile - 500 mg PO TID x10 to 14d*
Trichomoniasis - 2g PO single dose*
Bacterial vaginosis - Topical 0.75% gel 5g qd x5d. or Nonpregnant women 500 mg PO BID x 7 days OR 2 g PO qDay single dose, Pregnant women 500 mg PO BID x 7 days, OR 250 mg PO TID x 7 days*
Sexually Transmitted Disease , Prevention following sexual assault - 2 g PO as a single dose; 3 drug regimen that also includes ceftriaxone or cefixime, PLUS azithromycin or doxycycline*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): H. pylori - 500 mg PO BID x14d (triple therapy)*
Rosacea - 0.75% topical BID*
Prophylaxis; start after mechanical bowel preparation the afternoon and evening before surgery -1 g PO q6 to 8hr for 3 doses*
Nongonococcal Urethritis - 2 g PO qDay single dose with erythromycin (500 mg QID) or erythromycin ethylsuccinate (800 mg QID) x 7 days*
Pelvic Inflammatory Disease - 500 mg PO q12hr for 14 days in conjunction with ofloxacin or levofloxacin*

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Metronidazole + LITHIUM CARBONATE: Risk: Lithium toxicity (reduced renal clearance); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Lithium levels day 3 and 5; Dosage Adjustments: Reduce lithium by 30%; Alternative: Vancomycin for C.diff; Patient Warnings/Management: Report metallic taste/ataxia.
- Metronidazole + THIOPENTAL: Risk: ↓ Metronidazole efficacy + disulfiram-like reaction with IV thiopental. Monitoring: Ethanol screen, neurologic status. Dosage: Avoid ethanol-containing products; ↑ metronidazole 33%. Patient Warning: No alcohol 72h post-treatment.
- Metronidazole + PHENOBARBITAL: Risk: ↓ Metronidazole efficacy + ↑ neurotoxicity. Monitoring: Neurologic exam daily. Dosage: ↑ Metronidazole 50% or use IV formulation. Patient Warning: Report numbness/ataxia immediately.
- Metronidazole + PHENOBARBITONE: Risk: ↓ Metronidazole efficacy + ↑ neurotoxicity risk. Monitoring: Daily neurologic exams. Dosage: ↑ Metronidazole 50% or use IV route. Patient Warning: Absolute alcohol avoidance for 96h.
- Metronidazole + PRIMIDONE: Risk: Seizure threshold lowering + ↓ antibiotic efficacy. Monitoring: EEG if high doses. Dosage: Avoid combination in epilepsy patients. Patient Warning: Report myoclonus immediately.
- Metronidazole + AMOBARBITAL: Risk: ↓ Metronidazole efficacy + disulfiram-like reaction. Monitoring: Neurologic exam daily, ethanol screen. Dosage: ↑ Metronidazole 50%; avoid ethanol-containing products. Patient Warning: No alcohol 72h post-treatment.
- Metronidazole + PENTOBARBITAL: Risk: Seizure threshold lowering + neurotoxicity. Monitoring: EEG if high doses. Dosage: Avoid in epilepsy patients. Patient Warning: Report numbness/tingling immediately.
- Metronidazole + SECOBARBITAL: Risk: Psychotic reactions possible. Monitoring: Mental status exams. Dosage: ↑ Metronidazole 100% or avoid. Patient Warning: Report hallucinations/confusion.
- Metronidazole + PHENYTOIN: Risk: Metronidazole inhibits CYP2C9/19 → ↑ phenytoin levels (toxicity: nystagmus, ataxia); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Phenytoin levels at 48h & weekly; Dosage Adjustment: ↓ phenytoin 30-40% if combined >5 days; Alternative: Fosphenytoin (easier titration); Patient Warnings: Avoid alcohol (disulfiram-like react…
- Metronidazole + ANASTRAZOLE: Risk: CYP2C19 inhibition → anastrozole AUC↑150%. Monitoring: Breast tenderness/pain. Dose Adjustment: Anastrozole 0.5mg daily during combo. Patient Warning: Discontinue if metallic taste occurs.
- Metronidazole + WARFARIN: Risk: Severe INR elevation (CYP2C9 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: INR every 2-3 days during and 2 weeks post-therapy; Dosage: Reduce warfarin by 30-50% preemptively; Alternative: Amoxicillin; Patient Warnings: Avoid alcohol (disulfiram-like reaction), report dark urine/bleeding.
- Metronidazole + DICUMAROL: Risk: Marked INR ↑ (delayed by 3-7 days); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: INR q3d x2 weeks; Dosage: Reduce dicumarol by 40-50%; Alternative: Penicillin V; Patient Warnings: Report metallic taste (toxicity sign).
Also avoid duplicating the same actives in other cold/pain/antibiotic products.

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Avoid in 1st trimester if possible; may be used later when clearly indicated (e.g. trichomoniasis protocols) under obstetric advice.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Excreted in milk; may interrupt breastfeeding for high-dose courses or choose alternatives — follow obstetric/paediatric advice.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
