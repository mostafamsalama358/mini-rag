6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Xylometazoline + LINEZOLID: Risk: Extreme vasoconstriction (nasal decongestant + MAOI). Action: Contraindicated. Patient Warning: Use saline spray instead; report nosebleeds/vision changes.
- Xylometazoline + ISOCARBOXAZID: Risk: Hypertensive emergency. Action: Contraindicated. Patient Warning: Avoid all adrenergic stimulants (including OTC cold meds).
- Xylometazoline + PHENELZINE: Risk: Potentially fatal BP spike. Action: Contraindicated. Patient Warning: Check BP if accidental use; seek immediate care for severe headache.
- Xylometazoline + SELEGILINE CHLORIDE: Risk: Hypertension (even at low selegiline doses). Action: Contraindicated. Patient Warning: Transdermal selegiline >6 mg/24h carries highest risk.
- Xylometazoline + TRANYLCYPROMINE: Risk: Cerebrovascular hemorrhage. Action: Contraindicated. Patient Warning: Symptoms require ER visit (e.g., unilateral weakness).
- Xylometazoline + MOCLOBEMIDE: Risk: Pressor response (reversible MAOI). Action: Contraindicated. Patient Warning: Wait 48h after mocloemide cessation before use.
Also avoid duplicating the same actives in other cold/pain/antibiotic products.

7. Pregnancy

8. Breastfeeding
