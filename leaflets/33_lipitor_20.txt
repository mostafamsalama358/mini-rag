1. What Lipitor is used for
Catalog uses: antihyperlipidemic.statins
Approved uses (material sheet): Hyperlipidemia - Reduce elevated TC, LDL C, apoB, TG. Start 10 : 20 mg once daily; max 80 mg. Starting dose in patients requiring larger LDL C reduction (ie, >45%): 40 mg PO qDay , Maintenance: 10 : 80 mg PO qDay, check lipid levels after 2 : 4 weeks and adjust dose accordingly*
Hypertriglyceridemia, Adjunct to diet for elevated TG levels - 10 mg PO qDay initially , Maintenance: 10 to 80 mg PO qDay maintenance, check lipid levels after 2 : 4 weeks and adjust dose accordingly*
Reduction of risk of stroke, heart attack, and revascularization procedures in patients without evidence of coronary heart disease (CHD) but with multiple risk factors other than diabetes (eg, smoking, HTN, low HDL C, family history of early CHD)-10 : 80 mg PO qDay*
Primary Prevention - Reduce CV risk in adults with multiple risk factors*
Secondary Prevention- Reduce risk of MI, stroke in adults with CVD*

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Atorvastatin + AMIODARONE: Risk: ↑ Atorvastatin levels → myopathy; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: LFTs/CPK; Dosage Adjustments: Max 20mg daily; Alternative: Rosuvastatin; Patient Warnings/Management: Avoid strenuous exercise.
- Atorvastatin + DRONEDARONE: Risk: ↑ Atorvastatin levels (CYP3A4 inhibition) → myopathy; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: CPK if muscle symptoms; Dosage Adjustments: Max 20mg daily; Alternative: Rosuvastatin; Patient Warnings/Management: Report unexplained muscle pain.
- Atorvastatin + DIAZOXIDE: Risk: Exacerbated hyperglycemia; Type: Additive metabolic effect; Monitoring: Glucose HbA1c q3mo, LFTs; Dosage: Usually none needed; Alternative: Pravastatin; Patient: Standard diabetic/statin precautions.
- Atorvastatin + BOSENTAN: Risk: Reduced atorvastatin efficacy (CYP3A4 induction) & hepatotoxicity; Type: Pharmacokinetic & additive toxicity; Monitoring: Lipid panel, LFTs monthly; Dosage: ↑ Atorvastatin dose likely; Alternative: Fluvastatin; Patient: Report muscle pain.
- Atorvastatin + MINOXIDIL: Risk: Orthostatic hypotension; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: Sitting/standing BP, CK; Dosage: Minoxidil dose reduction may be needed; Alternative: Pravastatin; Patient: Rise slowly; report muscle pain.
- Atorvastatin + HYDRALAZINE: Risk: Hypotension; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: BP, LFTs, CK; Dosage: Usually none needed; Alternative: Pravastatin; Patient: Standard monitoring.
- Atorvastatin + ILOPROST: Risk: Hypotension; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: BP, LFTs, CK; Dosage: Usually none needed; Alternative: Pravastatin; Patient: Standard statin and iloprost precautions.
- Atorvastatin + CLOPIDOGREL: Risk: Reduced clopidogrel efficacy (CYP3A4 competition). Type: Pharmacokinetic. Monitoring: Platelet function tests. Dosage Adjustments: Max atorvastatin 20mg/day. Alternative: Rosuvastatin. Patient Warnings/Management: Report breakthrough angina; avoid grapefruit.
- Atorvastatin + AMLODIPINE: Risk: Rhabdomyolysis (3-fold ↑ atorvastatin AUC); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: CK at baseline/q3mo, LFTs; Dosage Adjustments: Atorvastatin ≤20mg daily; Alternative: Rosuvastatin; Patient Warnings/Management: Report muscle pain/weakness, dark urine.
- Atorvastatin + FELODIPINE: Risk: Hepatotoxicity (ALT >3x ULN); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: LFTs monthly x3; Dosage Adjustments: Atorvastatin ≤10mg daily; Alternative: Pitavastatin; Patient Warnings/Management: Report jaundice/fatigue.
- Atorvastatin + ISRADIPINE: Risk: Orthostatic myopathy; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: Postural CK changes; Dosage Adjustments: Atorvastatin ≤20mg; Alternative: Isradipine + colesevelam; Patient Warnings/Management: Rise slowly.
- Atorvastatin + NICARDIPINE: Risk: ICU myopathy; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Daily CK in ICU; Dosage Adjustments: Atorvastatin hold during infusion; Alternative: IV nicardipine + oral rosuvastatin; Patient Warnings/Management: Passive ROM exercises.
Also avoid duplicating the same actives in other cold/pain/antibiotic products.
