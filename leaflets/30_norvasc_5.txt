1. What Norvasc is used for
Catalog uses: antihypertensive.selective calcium channel blocker
Approved uses (material sheet): Hypertension - Adults: Start 5 mg daily; max 10 mg daily. Pediatrics (≥6 years): 2.5 : 5 mg daily*
Chronic Stable Angina - 5 : 10 mg daily*
Vasospastic Angina - 5 : 10 mg daily*
CAD - 5 : 10 mg daily to reduce hospitalization/revascularization*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Raynaud’s Phenomenon - 5 to 10 mg daily*
Diabetic Nephropathy - 5 to 10 mg daily with ACEI/ARB*
Group 1 PAH - 5 to 10 mg daily in vasoreactive patients*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): None for amlodipine. However, critical warnings include:
• Angina/MI Exacerbation: Rare worsening of ischemia at initiation or dose escalation, especially in severe CAD.
• Hypotension: Risk in volume-depleted or aortic stenosis patients
Notes (material sheet): • Avoid grapefruit products 
• Using amlodpine together with multivitamin with minerals can decrease the effects of amlodpine , You may need a dose adjustment or need your blood pressure checked more often if you take both medications (Calcium-containing products may decrease the effectiveness of calcium channel blockers by saturating calcium channels with calcium. Calcium chloride has been used to manage acute severe verapamil toxicity)
• Avoid natural licorice

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Calcium-channel blockers may be used when needed under obstetric advice.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Amlodipine appears in milk in small amounts; often considered acceptable with monitoring.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
