1. What Ventolin is used for
Catalog uses: bronchodilator
Approved uses (material sheet): Treatment and prevention of bronchospasm in patients with reversible obstructive airway disease - (e.g., asthma, exercise-induced bronchospasm); Dosing (MDI): Acute Bronchospasm: 2 puffs every 4 to 6 hours as needed , Exercise Induced: 2 puffs 15 to 30 minutes before exercise , Dosing (Nebulization): Adults & Children ≥12y: 2.5 mg 3 to 4 times daily as needed*
Prevention of exercise induced bronchospasm - Dosing (MDI): Acute Bronchospasm: 2 puffs every 4 to 6 hours as needed , Exercise Induced: 2 puffs 15 to 30 minutes before exercise , Dosing (Nebulization): Adults & Children ≥12y: 2.5 mg 3 to 4 times daily as needed*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Hyperkalemia in dialysis patients - Used to shift potassium into cells. Administered via nebulizer*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): • None for salbutamol itself.
(Note: Some fixed-dose combination inhalers containing long-acting beta-agonists (LABAs) do carry a Black Box Warning about an increased risk of asthma-related death, which led to the mandate that LABAs must always be used with an inhaled corticosteroid. This warning does not apply to short-acting relievers like salbutamol when used alone).

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Salbutamol inhalers are used in pregnancy when needed for asthma control — uncontrolled asthma is riskier.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Compatible; minimal milk levels.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.

10. Possible side effects
Major (material sheet): Nervousness - in children aged 2 to 6 years (>10%)*
Insomnia - in children aged 6 to 12 years receiving 4 to 12 mg q12hr (>10%)*
Tachycardia (>10%)*
Tremor - especially hand tremor (>10%)*
Headache (>10%)*
Minor (material sheet): Palpitations (1-5%)*
Muscle cramps (1-10%)*
Dizziness (1-5%)*
Irritability (1-5%)*
Dry mouth (1-7%)*
Hypokalemia - especially with high doses (1-3%)*
Paradoxical Bronchospasm - wheezing immediately after use; stop drug immediately (1-2%)*
