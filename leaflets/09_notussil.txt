1. What Notussil is used for
Catalog uses: anti-cough.non-productive
Approved uses (material sheet): Non Productive Cough - Adults: 60 mg PO TID , Children 2 to 14y: 1 mg/kg/dose TID (max 60mg/day)*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): COPD Associated Cough - 60 mg TID*
Post Infectious Cough - 60 mg BID to TID × 5d *

2. Dosage — Adults
Web-supplemented adult dosing: Egyptian Notussil-type: adults often 5 ml three times daily (confirm concentration on bottle). EU cloperastine 3.54 mg/ml syrups commonly 10 ml three times daily for ≥12 y.
Catalog DOSE field may still be empty — prefer this section + official Egyptian pack.

3. Dosage — Children
Web-supplemented paediatric dosing: Contraindicated under 2 years. Age-banded syrup dosing (examples for 3.54 mg/ml class): 2–4 y 2 ml BID; 5–6 y 3 ml BID; 7–12 y 5 ml BID–TID — always follow the Egyptian pack.
