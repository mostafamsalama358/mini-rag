6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Domperidone + ALUMINUM ASPIRIN: Risk: QT prolongation (QTc>450ms); Type: Additive cardiac effects; Monitoring: ECG baseline + weekly; Dosage Adjustments: Max domperidone 10mg TID; Alternative: Ginger supplements; Patient Warnings: Palpitation report
- Domperidone + ACETYL SALICYLIC ACID: Risk: Cardiac arrest; Type: Potassium channel blockade; Monitoring: K+ >4.0 required; Dosage Adjustments: Max 30mg/day; Alternative: Mosapride; Patient Warnings: Syncope danger
- Domperidone + ASPIRIN: Risk: Torsades de Pointes; Type: QTc prolongation (≥500ms); Monitoring: ECG baseline + weekly; Dosage Adjustments: Max 10mg TID; Alternative: Mosapride; Patient Warnings: Syncope danger
- Domperidone + TRIMETAZIDINE: Risk: EPS (SAS score >3). Type: Pharmacodynamic. Monitoring: Simpson-Angus Scale weekly. Dosage Adjustments: Avoid combination. Alternative: Ondansetron. Patient Warnings/Management: Report restlessness.
- Domperidone + DEXTROMETHORPHAN: Risk: Additive CNS depression via dopamine/sigma-1 receptor effects. Monitoring: Watch for excessive sedation, especially in CYP2D6 poor metabolizers. Dose Adjustment: Limit dextromethorphan to 30mg q6h if combined. Patient Warning: Avoid alcohol and driving. Mechanism: Weak opioid agonist with serotonergic activity.
- Domperidone + PENTAZOCINE: Risk: Mixed agonist-antagonist may reverse analgesia while increasing CNS depression. Monitoring: Pain scores and respiratory rate q4h. Dose Adjustment: Avoid combination - use pure agonist if needed. Patient Warning: May cause withdrawal in opioid-dependent patients. Mechanism: Kappa agonist/mu partial antagonist.
- Domperidone + TRAMADOL: Risk: Serotonin syndrome risk (weak SRI effect of tramadol). Monitoring: Watch for clonus, hyperreflexia. Dose Adjustment: Max tramadol 200mg/day if combined. Patient Warning: Report muscle twitching. Mechanism: Mu agonist + SNRI activity.
- Domperidone + CODEINE: Risk: Enhanced CNS depression in CYP2D6 ultrarapid metabolizers. Monitoring: Respiratory rate q2h initially. Dose Adjustment: Reduce codeine by 25-50%. Patient Warning: Watch for extreme sleepiness. Mechanism: Prodrug converted to morphine via CYP2D6.
- Domperidone + FENTANYL: Risk: Delayed gastric emptying increases transdermal absorption. Monitoring: Continuous pulse oximetry recommended. Dose Adjustment: Reduce fentanyl patch dose by 25%. Patient Warning: Remove patch if excessive drowsiness. Mechanism: Potent mu agonist.
- Domperidone + PETHIDINE (DEMEROL): Avoid combining domperidone with meperidine when possible due to additive risk of QT prolongation. Meperidine may reduce domperidone metabolism, raising its plasma concentration. Monitor ECG and neurologic status. Instruct patients to report any palpitations, dizziness, or syncope.
- Domperidone + KETOBEMIDONE: Risk: Enhanced dopamine blockade causing rigidity. Monitoring: Observe for Parkinsonian symptoms. Dose Adjustment: Avoid combination. Patient Warning: Report muscle stiffness. Mechanism: Mu agonist with NMDA antagonism.
- Domperidone + MORPHINE: Risk: Delayed gastric emptying increases oral bioavailability 2-fold. Monitoring: Respiratory depression for 12h post-dose. Dose Adjustment: Reduce oral morphine by 30-50%. Patient Warning: Take only when awake. Mechanism: Delayed GI transit increases absorption.
Also avoid duplicating the same actives in other cold/pain/antibiotic products.
