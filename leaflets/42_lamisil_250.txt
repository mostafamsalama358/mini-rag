1. What Lamisil is used for
Catalog uses: antifungals.allylamines
Approved uses (material sheet): Onychomycosis (Fingernail/Toenail) - 250 mg daily for 6 weeks (fingernails) or 12 weeks (toenails)*
Tinea Pedis (Athlete's Foot), Tinea Cruris (Jock Itch), Tinea Corporis (Ringworm) - Topical cream/gel/spray applied once or twice daily for 1 to 4 weeks*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Scalp Ringworm (Tinea Capitis) - In children, dosing is weight-based (e.g., 62.5 mg to 250 mg daily) for 2 to 4 weeks*
Pityriasis Versicolor - 250 mg daily for 1 to 2 weeks*
Fungal Keratitis - Used as an adjunctive topical preparation*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): Terbinafine does not carry a FDA Black Box Warning. 
However, the prescribing information contains strong warnings for:
• Hepatotoxicity:
- Cases of liver failure, some leading to death or liver transplant, have occurred.
• Serious Skin Reactions:
- (e.g., Stevens-Johnson Syndrome, Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis).
• Taste Disturbance:
- Including taste loss, which can be permanent.
Notes (material sheet): • Prevent premature labor
• Limit caffeine intake. Terbinafine may reduce the metabolism of caffeine by approximately 19%, 
• Take with or without food
• Avoid Nail Trauma:
- Wear protective footwear in public areas like pools and locker rooms.
• Do Not Share Personal Items:
- Such as nail clippers, files, or towels to prevent spread and reinfection.

Patient education excerpt: • Adherence:
- Take the tablet once daily, with or without food. Complete the full course as prescribed, even if the nail starts to look better.
• Time to See Results:
- Nail fungus clears slowly as the healthy nail grows out. It may take 6-12 months after stopping treatment to see the full result for toenails.
• Side Effects to Report Immediately:
- Liver: 
Loss of appetite, nausea, vomiting, unusual tiredness, yellowing of skin/eyes, dark urine.
- Taste/Smell:
 Any change or loss of taste or smell.
- Skin: 
Severe rash or blisters.
- Infection:
 Persistent fever, sore throat.

- Terbinafine + METOPROLOL: Risk: 2-4 fold increase in metoprolol exposure (CYP2D6 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Daily BP/HR for first week; Dosage: Reduce metoprolol by 50-75%; Alternative: Atenolol; Patient: Check pulse daily; rise slowly from sitting.
- Terbinafine + PROPRANOLOL: Risk: Markedly increased propranolol levels (CYP2D6/1A2 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP, CNS depression signs; Dosage: Reduce dose by 50% or switch to LA formulation; Alternative: Nadolol; Patient: Avoid driving until stable.
- Terbinafine + TIMOLOL: Risk: Systemic absorption potentiation → bradycardia; Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: IOP + resting HR; Dosage: Switch to 0.25% solution; Alternative: Brimonidine; Patient: Report fatigue/lightheadedness.
- Terbinafine + ACEBUTOLOL: Risk: Increased acebutolol/diacetolol levels (CYP2D6 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Exercise tolerance; Dosage: Reduce dose by 50%; Alternative: Bisoprolol; Patient: Document activity tolerance.
- Terbinafine + ATENOLOL: Risk: Minimal interaction (renal clearance); Type: Pharmacodynamic only; Monitoring: Routine BP; Dosage: None; Alternative: None needed; Patient: Normal precautions.
- Terbinafine + BETAXOLOL: Risk: Potentiated systemic effects (CYP2D6 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Pulmonary function in asthmatics; Dosage: Use 0.25% solution; Alternative: Prostaglandin analogs; Patient: Keep rescue inhaler available.
- Terbinafine + BISOPROLOL FUMARATE: Risk: Moderate increase in bisoprolol levels (partial CYP2D6 metabolism); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP/HR trends; Dosage: Reduce by 25-50% if needed; Alternative: Atenolol; Patient: Report excessive fatigue.
- Terbinafine + BISOPROLOL: Risk: Moderate increase in bisoprolol levels (partial CYP2D6 metabolism); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: BP/HR trends; Dosage: Reduce by 25-50% if needed; Alternative: Atenolol; Patient: Report excessive fatigue.
- Terbinafine + CARVEDILOL: Risk: 2-3 fold increase in carvedilol levels (CYP2D6 inhibition); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Orthostatic BP, HF symptoms; Dosage: Halve carvedilol dose; Alternative: Nebivolol; Patient: Daily weight checks.

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Prefer postpone oral terbinafine until after pregnancy unless essential.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Avoid oral terbinafine — excreted in milk.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
