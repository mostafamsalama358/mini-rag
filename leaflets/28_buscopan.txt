- Hyoscine + SODIUM NITROPRUSSIDE: Risk: Delirium, toxic megacolon (elderly); Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: RR, pupil dilation; Dosage: Avoid IV hyoscine; Alternative: Scopolamine patch (lower systemic exposure); Patient Warnings/Management: Supervise for agitation.
- Hyoscine + MEMANTINE: Risk: Sedation (RASS ≤-1). Type: Pharmacodynamic. Monitoring: Sedation scale q8h. Dosage Adjustments: ↓ HYOSCINE 50%. Alternative: Ondansetron. Patient Warnings/Management: Avoid alcohol.
