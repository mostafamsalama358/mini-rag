1. What Duphaston is used for
Catalog uses: -
Approved uses (material sheet): Luteal phase support - 10 mg BID starting at ovulation*
Dysmenorrhea - 10 mg BID days 5 : 25 of cycle*
Endometriosis - 10 mg BID , TID continuously*
HRT combination - 5 : 10 mg/day with estrogen*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Recurrent miscarriage - 10 mg BID until 20 weeks gestation*
