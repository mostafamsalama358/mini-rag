1. What Ferrotron is used for
Catalog uses: anemia.iron supplement combined with folic acid
Approved uses (material sheet): Iron deficiency anemia - 50 : 200 mg/day oral elemental iron*
CKD anemia - IV iron sucrose 100 mg/dialysis session*
Pregnancy prophylaxis - 27 mg/day*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Restless Legs Syndrome - 50 to 100 mg/day oral (target ferritin >75 µg/L)*
Heart failure - IV ferric carboxymaltose 15 mg/kg*
Breath holding attacks (children) - 1 to 2 mg/kg/day*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): • Pediatric Overdose Risk:
-Accidental ingestion of >60 mg/kg may cause fatal metabolic acidosis. Store in child-proof containers
Notes (material sheet): • Indicated for treatment of iron deficiency anemia associated with chronic kidney disease
• Iron absorption is inhibited by eggs , milk , Cocoa and coffee , drink a lot of tea with your meals , Walnuts (contain phytates) , soy protein and fiber , medications that decrease the amount of acid in the stomach, including antacids and proton pump inhibitors (PPIs)
• Some products that may interact with this drug include: vitamin products, other iron-containing products.
• Foods rich in iron include meats (especially liver), raisins, figs, broccoli, brussels sprouts, beans, lentils, and iron-fortified or enriched cereals
• Avoid:
- CALCIUM OR CALCIUM CARBONATE / ZINC OR ZINC GLUCONATE supplements…
Patient education excerpt: • Timing: 
-Take 1 hr before meals or 2 hrs after for best absorption; with food if GI upset occurs.
• Food Interactions: 
-Avoid dairy, tea, coffee within 2 hrs; take with vitamin C-rich foods.
• Missed Dose: 
-Skip if near next dose; never double dose.
• Overdose Antidote: 
-Deferoxamine IV .

- Iron + COLESTIPOL: Risk: Severe iron absorption reduction (50-70%) → refractory anemia; Type: Pharmacokinetic (GI binding); Monitoring: FBC weekly, iron studies monthly; Dosage: Strict 6h separation (AM iron/PM colestipol); Alternative: IV iron (ferric carboxymaltose/ferumoxytol); Patient Warnings/Management: Take iron with 250mg vitami…
- Iron + COLESEVELAM: Risk: Moderate absorption decrease (30-50%); Type: Pharmacokinetic (GI binding); Monitoring: FBC every 3-4 weeks; Dosage: Minimum 4h separation; Alternative: Ferric maltol if compliance issues; Patient Warnings/Management: Morning iron/evening colesevelam preferred.
- Iron + ENTACAPONE: Risk: Reduced entacapone absorption → wearing-off; Monitoring: "Off" time diaries; Dosage: Separate by 4h; increase levodopa if needed; Alternative: IV iron if severe deficiency; Patient: Take iron with orange juice at night.
- Iron + LEVODOPA: Risk: Chelation → ↓ levodopa absorption (up to 50%) worsening Parkinsonism; Monitoring: "ON/OFF" diaries, serum ferritin; Dosage: ↑ levodopa by 100-200mg/day if needed; Patient: Take levodopa 1h before meals, iron 2h after meals with OJ; avoid antacids.
- Iron + LEVOTHYROXINE: Risk: Iron reduces levothyroxine absorption by 30-50% when coadministered. Monitoring: TSH 6 weeks after iron initiation/dose change. Dosage Adjustment: Administer levothyroxine 4 hours before iron. Patient Warning: Take levothyroxine on empty stomach with water only.
- Iron + MOXIFLOXACIN: Moxifloxacin chelates iron in the gut, reducing antibiotic absorption by ~30–50% and risking subtherapeutic exposure; separate doses by ≥2 h, monitor infection response, and instruct patients to space iron supplements away from antibiotic dosing.
- Iron + CIPROFLOXACIN: Iron salts chelate ciprofloxacin, reducing bioavailability by ~30–50% and risking therapeutic failure; separate administration by ≥2 h, monitor clinical signs of infection, and counsel patients to stagger iron and antibiotic doses.
- Iron + NORFLOXACIN: Norfloxacin and iron may form insoluble complexes, reducing antibiotic absorption byup to 50%; separate doses by4–6 hours; monitor for inadequate infection resolution; advise patients to avoid concurrent administration and report persistent symptoms (e.g., fever, pain).
Also avoid duplicating the same actives in other cold/pain/antibiotic products.

10. Possible side effects
Major (material sheet): Nausea (15–30%)*
Constipation (20%)*
Dark stools (100%)*
Minor (material sheet): Anaphylaxis - IV iron (0.1–1%)*
Iron overload - ferritin >1,000 ng/mL (1%)*
Hypophosphatemia - IV formulations (1%)*
