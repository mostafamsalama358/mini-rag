5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): • None
Notes (material sheet): • Cinnarizine interferes with the allergy test and gives a false result because there is no allergy to penicillins despite the presence of allergy. The solution, as mentioned in the scientific sources, is to stop the Cinnarizine drug 4 days before the allergy test 
• he must have a probiotic such as lacteol fort cap for use after it and advise him to eat prebiotic-rich food such as garlic, onions, bananas, yogurt
• Avoid taking too many calcium-enriched foods and drinks as it might affect the working of AMOXICILLIN.
• Avoid bacteriostatic agents (e.g., tetracyclines) which may antagonize effects
• Take with food.

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Amoxicillin + METHOTREXATE: Risk: Competitive tubular secretion → 60% ↑ MTX AUC. Monitoring: Pre/post-dose creatinine clearance. Dose Adjustment: MTX ≤15mg/week + IV hydration. Patient Warning: Report ↓ urine output immediately.
- Amoxicillin + WARFARIN: Risk: Minimal INR change (occasional GI flora effect); Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: INR baseline + weekly; Dosage: None typically; Alternative: Cephalexin; Patient Warnings: Report rash (mononucleosis contraindication).
- Amoxicillin + DICUMAROL: Risk: Minimal INR change; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: INR baseline + weekly; Dosage: None; Alternative: Cephalexin; Patient Warnings: Maintain consistent vitamin K intake.
- Amoxicillin + PHENPROCOUMON: Risk: Minimal INR change; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: INR baseline + weekly; Dosage: None; Alternative: Cephalexin; Patient Warnings: Maintain consistent vitamin K intake.
- Amoxicillin + ACENOCOUMAROL: Risk: Minimal INR change; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: INR baseline + weekly; Dosage: None; Alternative: Cephalexin; Patient Warnings: Maintain consistent vitamin K intake.
- Amoxicillin + ALLOPURINOL: Risk: Maculopapular rash (22% incidence, higher in renal impairment); Type: Hypersensitivity; Monitoring: Skin exam daily for 14 days; Dosage: Consider alternative antibiotics; Alternative: Cefuroxime; Patient Warnings: Discontinue at first sign of rash.
- Amoxicillin + PROBENECID: Risk: Maculopapular rash (↑ amoxicillin levels); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Skin exam; Dosage: Reduce amoxicillin by 25%; Alternative: Cefuroxime; Patient Warnings: Discontinue at first rash.
- Amoxicillin + SULFINPYRAZONE: Risk: Maculopapular rash (↑ amoxicillin levels); Type: Pharmacokinetic; Monitoring: Skin exam; Dosage: Reduce amoxicillin by 25%; Alternative: Cefuroxime; Patient Warnings: Discontinue at first rash.
- Amoxicillin + DABIGATRAN ETEXILATE: Risk: Potential increased bleeding risk due to altered gut flora affecting dabigatran absorption. Type: Pharmacokinetic (indirect). - Amoxicillin + APIXABAN: Risk: Theoretical bleeding risk due to gut microbiome changes. Type: Indirect pharmacokinetic. Monitoring: No INR needed; monitor for clinical bleeding. Dosage: Standard apixaban dosing. Alternative: Azithromycin for respiratory infections. Patient Warnings: Use soft-bristle toothbrush; avoid NSAIDs.
- Amoxicillin + RIVAROXABAN: Risk: Minimal interaction but possible bleeding risk. Type: Unclear mechanism. Monitoring: Anti-Xa levels if available; clinical bleeding assessment. Dosage: No adjustment needed. Alternative: Doxycycline for skin infections. Patient Warnings: Report headaches or swelling (signs of hemorrhage).
- Amoxicillin + EDOXABAN: Risk: Low clinical significance. Type: Theoretical. Monitoring: Clinical bleeding signs; renal function. Dosage: Edoxaban 30 mg if CrCl 15-50 mL/min. Alternative: Nitrofurantoin for UTIs. Patient Warnings: Avoid falls/trauma; monitor gum bleeding.
