1. What Amaryl is used for
Catalog uses: anti-diabetic.secretagogues.sulfonylurea
Approved uses (material sheet): Type 2 diabetes - (1 : 8 mg/day monotherapy or with insulin/metformin)*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): MODY (Maturity Onset Diabetes of the Young) - Limited evidence for subtypes 1/3*
Cardioprotection - Less impact on ischemic preconditioning vs. other sulfonylureas*

2. Dosage — Adults
Web-supplemented adult dosing: Usually start 1 mg once daily with breakfast; titrate. Maintenance often 1–4 mg/day (max commonly 6–8 mg). Hypoglycaemia risk.

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): • None for glimepiride, 
• but sulfonylureas carry a class warning for increased cardiovascular mortality (based on UGDP study)
Notes (material sheet): • Avoid alcohol. Acute and chronic alcohol intake may unpredictably affect the glucose-lowering action of glimepiride
• Avoid with bosentan:
-Contraindicated due to hepatotoxicity risk.
• Herbal interactions: 
-Bitter melon/fenugreek may potentiate hypoglycemia
• Take with food. 
The manufacturer recommends administration with the first meal of the day
• If you are taking colesevelam, take glimepiride at least 4 hours before taking colesevelam
• Check the labels on all your medicines (such as cough-and-cold products) because they may contain ingredients that could affect your blood sugar.

7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Avoid sulfonylureas; switch to insulin protocols in pregnancy.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Avoid — hypoglycaemia risk to infant.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
