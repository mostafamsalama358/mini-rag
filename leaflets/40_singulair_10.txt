1. What Singulair is used for
Catalog uses: asthma.anti-inflammatory.leukotriene antagonist
Approved uses (material sheet): Asthma prophylaxis - 10 mg daily (adults); 5 mg (6 to 14y); 4 mg (2 to 5y)*
Allergic rhinitis - 10 mg daily: If Patients with both asthma and allergic rhinitis should take only 1 dose qPM*
Exercise induced bronchospasm - 10 mg 2h pre exercise*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Chronic urticaria - 10 mg daily (second-line)*
Eosinophilic esophagitis - 10 mg daily*
Aspirin exacerbated respiratory disease, including aspirin challenge/desensitization (adjunct) - Oral: 10 mg once daily in the evening*

5. Warnings and precautions
BLACK BOX / serious warnings (material sheet): Serious neuropsychiatric events that may include suicidal thoughts or actions have been reported in patients taking montelukast, If changes in behavior are observed, or aggression, depression, and sleep disturbances reported, advise patients to discontinue montelukast, especially in children. This Events have occurred in patients with and without pre-existing psychiatric disease

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Montelukast + THIOPENTAL: Risk: THIOPENTAL decreases MONTELUKAST levels by 30% via CYP3A4 induction. Monitor: Asthma control tests. Dosage: MONTELUKAST may need 50% dose increase. Patient Warning: Watch for worsening wheezing.
- Montelukast + PHENOBARBITAL: Risk: PHENOBARBITAL decreases MONTELUKAST levels by 40% via CYP3A4 induction. Monitor: Asthma control test scores monthly. Dosage: Increase MONTELUKAST to 15mg nightly if >15yo. Patient Warning: Watch for nocturnal asthma symptoms.
- Montelukast + PHENOBARBITONE: Risk: PHENOBARBITONE reduces MONTELUKAST efficacy by 50%. Monitor: Fractional exhaled nitric oxide (FeNO) levels. Dosage: Consider alternative leukotriene modifier. Patient Warning: Increased rescue inhaler use may be needed.
- Montelukast + PRIMIDONE: Risk: PRIMIDONE (via phenobarbital) decreases MONTELUKAST AUC by 60%. Monitor: Morning/evening peak flow variability. Dosage: MONTELUKAST may need 2x daily dosing. Patient Warning: Report increased wheezing at night.
- Montelukast + AMOBARBITAL: Risk: Reduced montelukast efficacy (CYP3A4/CYP2C9 induction) worsening asthma control. Monitor: Asthma symptoms, PEFR/FEV1, rescue inhaler use. Dose Adjust: Consider montelukast dose increase if control declines; Patient Warning: Report increased wheezing/shortness of breath immediately; never stop asthma meds without…
- Montelukast + PENTOBARBITAL: Risk: Significantly reduced montelukast exposure (CYP induction) compromising asthma management. Monitor: Day/night symptom scores, exacerbation frequency. Dose Adjust: Optimize asthma therapy; consider alternative controller; Patient Warning: Use rescue inhaler for breakthrough symptoms; attend all follow-ups.
- Montelukast + SECOBARBITAL: Risk: Loss of asthma control from reduced montelukast AUC. Monitor: ACT/C-ACT scores, exacerbations. Dose Adjust: Consider high-dose montelukast (only if >15yrs) or switch therapy; Patient Warning: Carry emergency epinephrine if history of anaphylaxis; track symptom triggers.
- Montelukast + ANASTRAZOLE: Risk: CYP2C8 competition → anastrozole levels↑. Monitoring: Morning trough levels. Dose Adjustment: Evening montelukast dosing. Patient Warning: Mood changes significant.
