6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)
- Folic Acid + THIOPENTAL: Risk: ↓ Antiepileptic efficacy (enhanced metabolism) → breakthrough seizures. Monitor: Seizure frequency, EEG if indicated. Dose Adjust: ↑ Thiopental dose by 20-30% if levels subtherapeutic. Patient: Report aura/seizure recurrence; maintain medication adherence.
- Folic Acid + PHENOBARBITAL: Risk: ↓ Phenobarbital levels → seizure recurrence (CYP induction by folate). Monitor: Phenobarbital levels q1-2mo, seizure diary. Dose Adjust: ↑ Phenobarbital by 25% if levels <15 mcg/mL. Patient: Never skip doses; report increased seizures or mood changes.
- Folic Acid + PHENOBARBITONE: Risk: ↓ Antiepileptic efficacy → loss of seizure control. Monitor: Serum levels (target 15-40 mcg/mL), breakthrough seizures. Dose Adjust: ↑ Phenobarbitone by 5-10mg/wk guided by levels. Patient: Use seizure precautions; report injury-causing events.
- Folic Acid + PRIMIDONE: Risk: ↓ Primidone/phenobarbital levels → increased seizure risk. Monitor: Phenobarbital levels (from primidone metabolism), seizure frequency. Dose Adjust: ↑ Primidone by 25% if phenobarbital <15 mcg/mL. Patient: Watch for tremor/nystagmus (toxicity when folate stopped).
- Folic Acid + AMOBARBITAL: Risk: Reduced antiepileptic effect → status epilepticus risk. Monitor: Amobarbital levels (if available), seizure clusters. Dose Adjust: ↑ Amobarbital by 30-50mg/day if control wanes. Patient: Carry rescue medication; report >1 breakthrough seizure/week.
- Folic Acid + PENTOBARBITAL: Risk: ↓ Antiepileptic efficacy → increased hospitalization risk. Monitor: Seizure severity/duration, serum levels. Dose Adjust: ↑ Pentobarbital by 20% with level monitoring. Patient: Document triggers; attend all neurology appointments.
- Folic Acid + SECOBARBITAL: Risk: Subtherapeutic levels → withdrawal seizures. Monitor: Withdrawal symptoms, level checks after folate initiation. Dose Adjust: ↑ Secobarbital by 25-50mg if levels drop >25%. Patient: Taper gradually if discontinuing; report anxiety/tremors.
- Folic Acid + PHENYTOIN: Risk: Reduced phenytoin efficacy → breakthrough seizures. Monitoring: Serum phenytoin monthly; RBC folate. Dosage: Limit folate to ≤1 mg/day; increase phenytoin as needed. Patient: Take folate at opposite time of day from phenytoin.
- Folic Acid + SULFASALAZINE: Risk: Sulfasalazine inhibits folate absorption → ↓ levels by 40-60%. Monitoring: RBC folate levels q3mo; homocysteine if deficient. Dose Adjustment: Folic acid 5mg daily (instead of 1mg). Patient Warning: Take folate 2h before sulfasalazine.
- Folic Acid + FEBUXOSTAT: Risk: Masked B12 deficiency; Type: Pharmacodynamic; Monitoring: MMA levels; Dosage: No adjustment; Alternative: Folinic acid; Patient Warnings: Neurological checks.
Also avoid duplicating the same actives in other cold/pain/antibiotic products.
