7. Pregnancy
Web-supplemented pregnancy guidance: Avoid in 3rd trimester (ductus arteriosus / oligohydramnios risk). Avoid if possible in 1st/2nd unless doctor directs.

8. Breastfeeding
Web-supplemented breastfeeding guidance: Prefer avoid NSAIDs or use short courses only with clinician advice; ibuprofen often preferred if NSAID needed.

9. Supplements / cross-selling
Catalog cross-selling: None listed
Supportive tips: separate minerals from quinolones/tetracyclines by 2–4 hours; probiotics may be timed away from antibiotics; ORS with antidiarrheals when dehydrated.
