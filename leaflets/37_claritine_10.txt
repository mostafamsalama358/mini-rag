1. What Claritine is used for
Catalog uses: anti-histamine.anti-allergy
Approved uses (material sheet): Allergic rhinitis - 10 mg/day (adults); 5 mg/day (ages 2 to 5)*
Chronic urticaria - 10 mg/day*
Off-label mentions (material sheet): Pruritus in eczema/dermatitis - 10 mg/day*
Kimura’s disease - (itch relief)*

6. Interactions (from NEW DRUG INTERACTION.xlsx)

- Loratadine + DEXTROMETHORPHAN: Risk: Cognitive impairment; Interaction: Subtle CNS synergy; Monitoring: Digit symbol test; Dosage: Space doses 6h apart; Alternative: Beclomethasone nasal; Patient: Avoid complex tasks.
