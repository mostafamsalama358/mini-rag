# Re-index Compatibility Policy

**Authority**: Architecture Decision D7 / research R-010

When the sole chunking / embed-text policy changes in a way that materially alters indexed content:

1. **Preferred**: Complete re-index for affected corpora before declaring M4/M7 complete for that policy.
2. **Alternative**: Record an explicit **waiver** with risk acceptance (who, when, scope, residual risk).
3. **Forbidden**: Silent corpus skew with neither re-index nor waiver.
